## 1. 2024 한부모가족 실태조사: survey design and headline figures (income, debt, housing, work)

### Takeaway
The full report confirms almost every headline figure in the earlier notes:
- income 294.6만원 (모자 250.6 / 부자 325.3);
- debt 4,720.7만원;
- 자가 23.6%;
- 30.8% temporary or day workers;
- 66.9% name cash as the #1 need.

Several framings need fixing, though:
- The fieldwork period was wrong.
- "83.9% 경제활동률" is really an employment rate.
- Net assets are a derived figure.
- The average weekly hours is 40.0. The summary's "50–52시간" is a typo for 40–52 hours.

The survey covers only single parents with children aged 18 or under, about 35.5만 households. It does not cover the 149만 census 한부모가구.

### Cited Findings
**Survey design** [1차-보고서, 연구요약 표1·표3]
- n = 3,315 heads of single-parent households with a child aged 18 or under, interviewed in person at home.
- **Fieldwork: 2024-06 to 2024-08**, reference date 2024-06-01. Fieldwork by 한국갤럽; lead agency 한국여성정책연구원; 통계청 승인 제154016호.
- Sampling frame: 2022 등록센서스. In 2022 there were **35.5만 한부모가구 with children aged 18 or under**. That is 1.63% of all households and 7.57% of households with minor children. — [보고서](https://clik.nanet.go.kr/clikr-collection/policyinfo/203/1016/2025/CLIKC2222284649985253_attach_1.pdf)

### Inferences
- The two biggest 모자 vs 부자 gaps are housing (자가 14.7% vs 32.0%) and 양육비 (see §3).

- Housing debt is concentrated in father-headed households (62.2% of borrowers), while mother-headed households borrow for living costs (46.0%). In other words, mothers' debt is a symptom of poverty, and fathers' debt is a sign of asset-building.

## 2. 2024 survey: care, children's time alone, social support, discrimination, emotional health (모자 vs 부자)

### Takeaway
- Childcare strain is real but is concentrated among preschool and elementary-age children:
  - finding someone to look after preschoolers: 66.7% (모자 74.7%);
  - 35.9% of elementary-age children have time alone after school, rising to 50.6% in 부자 households.
- Isolation is the strongest "non-cash" signal. 20–45% have no one to turn to, and this is up from previous surveys. Single parents without grandparents in the household are far more isolated than those with grandparents (48.0% vs 10.8% have no help with housework).
- Depression measured with PHQ-9 is **7.3%**, not the 20.2% cited earlier.
- Fathers cope by drinking (38.6%) far more than mothers (9.0%).
- Discrimination fell sharply across every area.

### Cited Findings
**Childcare and time alone** [1차-보고서, 연구요약 2장, 표Ⅱ-2-5, 표Ⅱ-2-11] — [보고서](https://clik.nanet.go.kr/clikr-collection/policyinfo/203/1016/2025/CLIKC2222284649985253_attach_1.pdf)
- Difficulties raising a preschool child (% answering "그렇다"):
  - cost of childcare and education 78.0%;
  - **finding someone to look after the child 66.7%** (the table shows 66.8%; 모자 74.7%, 모자+기타 57.2%, 부자 69.3%, 부자+기타 52.3%);
  - **lack of time for childcare 63.9%**;
  - lack of information 53.0%.
- Cost burden is the top difficulty at every stage: preschool 78.0%, elementary 81.4%, middle/high school 82.1%.
- Time alone on weekdays with no adult present:
  - Preschool children: only **3.2%** have any time alone. Their average is 2.2 hours.
  - Elementary children: **35.9%** have time alone, averaging 2.5 hours. By type: 모자 40.7%, 부자 50.6%, 모자+기타 16.7%, 부자+기타 20.4%.

### Inferences
- The core care need is **evening and after-school coverage for elementary-age children in households without grandparents**. Half of 부자-only households have a child alone after school. Wanted services cluster around meals, night-time care and drop-off/pick-up.
- Emotional need is under-served rather than high-prevalence:
  - the PHQ-9 rate is moderate (7.3%);
  - almost no one uses professional help (0.1%);
  - fathers rely heavily on alcohol.
  - This suggests low-threshold, non-clinical formats such as peer groups and practical support, particularly for fathers.
- "Grandparent present" is a stronger divider of isolation than mother vs father. Help with housework: none for 48.0% in 모자 vs 10.8% in 모자+기타.

### Gaps
- No suicidal-ideation item was found in the 2024 survey summary.
- The 20.2% vs 10.3% depression figure from the earlier notes could not be traced to the 2024 survey. It likely uses a different instrument or year.
- 부자 vs 모자 splits for the childcare-time item and for elementary-school difficulties were not extracted. They are in 표Ⅱ-2-9 and 2-14~19 if needed.

## 3. 양육비: survey receipt rates, 양육비이행관리원 statistics, 선지급제 results

### Takeaway
- **71.3%** of divorced or unmarried single parents get no child support. This splits sharply by sex: **모자 63.6% vs 부자 89.0%**.
- **33.3%** hold a legal 양육비 claim (채권). Among those with a **regular-payment** claim, 80.1% actually receive (63.8% regularly + 16.3% irregularly). Among those **without any claim**, 2.6% receive.
- 양육비이행관리원 results:
  - cumulative collections reached 2,200억 (2024), 2,719억 (2025) and 3,002억 (2026-06);
  - consultations rose from 37,142 (2024) to 65,978 (2025);
  - compliance rate about 49.9% (2026-06).
- 선지급제 through 2026-09: 8,514 households, 13,442 children, about 253억원. Recovery of the 2025 H2 payments: 14.66% by amount and 23.62% by cases.

### Inferences
- Non-payment is close to total for 부자 households (89% receive nothing, and 60.6% agreed at divorce that no support would be paid). The 양육비 problem is therefore largely a **mother-headed-household** problem in cash terms. Fathers who need support face a norm of "not asking".
- Use of the 관리원 is tiny (4.1%), and willingness to use it collapsed (17% → 6.7%) even before 선지급. The 2025 jump in consultations (+78%) coincides with 선지급's launch. The top reasons for not using it, "the ex can't pay" and "don't want contact", point to demand for a state-guaranteed payment that requires no contact with the ex. That is exactly what 선지급 offers.
- Recovery is low and was budgeted for less than half the actual take-up in year one. With the income test gone, scheme costs will rise faster than recovery.

## 5. 정정 (corrections and caveats to the earlier notes `divorced_single_parent_needs.md`)

### Takeaway
Most headline numbers survive primary verification. The main fixes are:
1. **Fieldwork dates.**
2. **The depression rate.** It is 7.3% (PHQ-9), not 20.2%.
3. **The 양육비 trend framing.** The comparable series is 78.9% → 80.7% → 71.3%, a real decline, not "barely changed".
4. **The 80.1% / 2.6% denominators.**
5. **"Economic activity rate" should read employment rate.**
6. **The discrimination figure** at schools is 5.7%, not 5.6%.
7. **"No 부자 vs 모자 splits exist".** They do, and they are large.

### Cited Findings
All items are checked against [보고서](https://clik.nanet.go.kr/clikr-collection/policyinfo/203/1016/2025/CLIKC2222284649985253_attach_1.pdf) unless another source is named.

1. **Fieldwork period.** The earlier notes said "2024-03 to 12". The correct period is **2024-06 to 2024-08** (reference date 2024-06-01) [1차-보고서 표1].
2. **Depression.** The earlier notes gave "20.2% vs 10.3%" as the 한부모 depression rate. The 2024 survey's PHQ-9 rate is **7.3%** (모자 8.7%, 부자 4.7%). The 20.2% figure could not be traced to the 2024 survey [1차-보고서 표Ⅱ-6-2].
3. **양육비 trend.** The earlier notes said "73.1% → 72.1% → 71.3%, barely changed". That compares 2018/2021 "never received" with the 2024 "received nothing" measure. The report's comparable non-receipt series is **78.9% → 80.7% → 71.3%**, a fall of 7.6–9.4%p [1차-보고서 연구요약 4장].
4. **80.1% vs 2.6%.** The earlier notes said "80.1% receive with a claim". The denominator is holders of a **regular-payment** claim (n = 917), not all 33.3% of claim holders.
   - The 헤럴드 breakdown (63.8% regular + 16.3% irregular) is **correct**. The earlier notes wrongly called it an error.
   - 2.6% = 0.4% + 2.2% among those with no claim (n = 1,966) [1차-보고서 표Ⅱ-4-16, 4-19].
5. **Employment.** The earlier notes gave "economic activity rate 83.9% vs 69.2%". 83.9% is the **share currently employed**, and the 69.2% benchmark is the 15–64 **employment rate** [1차-보고서 표Ⅱ-5-1].
6. **Discrimination at schools.** "21.1% → 5.6%" should be **5.7%** for parents (children: 19.2% → 6.5%) [1차-보고서].
7. **Children's time alone.** "Preschool 2.2h / elementary 2.5h per day" applies only to the 3.2% of preschoolers and 35.9% of elementary children who have any time alone. It is not a population average [1차-보고서].
8. **Gap claim: "no 2024 부자 vs 모자 split for needs, depression, hours, debt".** The report has all of them (§1–3 above):
   - hours: 모자 38.3 / 부자 42.2;
   - debt: 3,940.7 / 5,577.7만원;
   - #1 need, cash: 72.5% / 57.7%;
   - depression: 8.7% / 4.7%.
9. **Gap claim: "only 모자 68.7% seen".** 68.7% = 모자 53.5 + 모자+기타 15.2. Father-headed households total **31.3%** (20.3 + 11.0) [1차-보고서 표Ⅱ-1-5].
10. **Net assets 1억1,568만원 (25.8%).** Confirmed, but the figure is **derived** (financial assets + real estate − debt). The report does not print it.
    - The 2021 comparison in the report is net assets of 10,950만원, 26.4% of the all-household average. So the ratio slipped slightly, from 26.4% to about 25.8% [1차-보고서 p.2 서론].
11. **Working hours.** The report summary says "73.8%가 50–52시간 범주". The table shows **40–52 hours**, so the summary contains a typo. Do not quote "50–52" [1차-보고서 표Ⅱ-5-8].
12. **Childcare difficulty 66.7%.** The text says 66.7% and the table says 66.8%. Either is fine; cite 66.7% as the report text [1차-보고서 표Ⅱ-2-11].
13. **선지급 recovery.** "9.4% at the one-year mark" (end-June 2026) and "14.66% at end-September" are both correct, at different dates. There is an intermediate 10.3% at 2026-07-17. The 12.94% (excluding extinguished claims) remains [스니펫] — [경향](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202610301601001); [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260721_0003717193)
14. **"Census 149만 한부모가구" vs the survey universe.** The survey universe is 35.5만 households with a child aged 18 or under (2022 등록센서스). Do not apply the survey's percentages to the 149만 figure, which includes adult children [1차-보고서 서론].
15. **2025 divorce figures** (88K; 37K with minor children, 42.5%; 15,628 vs 14,392): **confirmed** [1차-통계]. The exact count for minor-children divorces is 37,441, so "3.74만" is the precise rounding. The 60+ couple count of 13,743 remains unverified.
