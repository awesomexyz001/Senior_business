## 1. Size, trend and projections (count/share of 노인 부부가구; couple → widowhood transition)

### Takeaway
In the 2023 노인실태조사, couple-only living is still the most common arrangement: **55.2%** of people aged 65+ live in a 부부가구 (the 48.4% in the brief is not the right figure). Its share is falling fast as one-person households jump (19.8% → 32.8%, 2020→2023). Counted by household head, couple households were **35.0%** of 65+-headed households in 2022, projected at **34.4%** in 2052. Because 65+-headed households more than double over that period, the absolute number of elderly couple households roughly doubles even though its share stays flat.

### Cited Findings
- **2023 노인실태조사** (MOHW/KIHASA; fieldwork 2023-09-04 to 11-12; n=10,078 aged 65+; released 2024-10-16): household types were **부부가구 55.2%, 1인 가구 32.8%, 자녀동거 10.3%**. One-person households rose 13.0%p from 2020. Average household size fell from 2.0 to 1.8. **[V]** — [MOHW press release PDF via korea.kr](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156655081); also [MOHW board](https://www.mohw.go.kr/board.es?mid=a10503010100&bid=0027&act=view&list_no=1483352)
  - 2020 comparison: 독거 **19.8%** (2020) → 32.8% (2023) **[V]** — [Segye 2026-08-06](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260806512102). 자녀동거 20.1% (2020) → 10.3% (2023) **[V]** — press release above.
  - **Note on "48.4%":** this figure does not appear in the 2023 press release. Nearby numbers are 48.6% (couples who rate themselves "건강하다") and 48.1% (couples reporting "생활상의 어려움"). The 2020 couple share was probably about 58.4%, but that comes from my own memory, not a source, so it must be checked. **[I]**
- **Couple households compared with solo elderly (2023 노인실태조사)** **[V]** — [press release](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156655081)

  | 지표 | 독거노인 | 노인부부 | 자녀동거 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 주관적 건강 "건강하다" | 34.2% | 48.6% | 43.2% |
  | 우울증상 | 16.1% | 7.8% | 15.0% |
  | 평균 만성질환 | 2.4개 | 2.0개 | 2.2개 |
  | 영양관리 "양호" | 54.4% | 71.2% | 64.1% |
  | 생활상의 어려움 있다 | 73.9% | 48.1% | – |

  Couples are clearly better off than solo elderly. Still, about half report daily-life difficulty, and couples are the pool that solo households are drawn from.
- **장래가구추계 2022–2052** (Statistics Korea, released 2024-09-12):
  - 65+-headed households grow from **522.5만 (2022) to 1,178.8만 (2052)**, ×2.3, rising from 24.1% to 50.6% of all households. **[S]** — [NewDaily 2024-09-12](https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2024/09/12/2024091200210.html)
  - Composition of 65+-headed households: 2022 = 1인 36.8%, **부부 35.0%**, 부부+자녀 9.2%; 2052 = 1인 42.1%, **부부 34.4%**, 부부+자녀 7.1%. **[S]** — [NewDaily](https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2024/09/12/2024091200210.html), [Newsis 2024-12-12 (시도편)](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20241212_0002993974)
  - Couple households of all ages go from 17.3% (374만) in 2022 to 22.8% (532만) in 2052. The increase is driven by longer life expectancy and older couples. **[S]** — same sources
- **[I] Calculated counts:** elderly couple households ≈ 522.5만 × 35.0% ≈ **183만 (2022)**, rising to ≈ 1,178.8만 × 34.4% ≈ **405만 (2052)**, about ×2.2. Person-level estimate: 1,051.4만 people aged 65+ in 2025 ([Newsis 2025-09-29 고령자통계, S](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20250929_0003347644)) × 55.2% ≈ **580만 older adults living as couples**, or very roughly 300만 couple households. This is an upper-bound style figure, because some spouses are under 65.
- **The shift toward living alone is already visible:** 65+ one-person households reached 245.6만 in the 2025 인구주택총조사, up 7.3% year on year, or 10.9% of all general households. **[S]** — [Newsis 2025-12-09](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251209_0003433761). Women make up 165.5만 of the elderly one-person households (2025), and 40.1% of women living alone are 65+. **[S]** — [Supple / Segye 2026-01-04](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260104504884)
- **Widowhood is mostly female:** among long-term-care recipients, 70.9% are women and 71.4% are 80+. In 2025, 43.6% of home-care recipients lived alone (+3.4%p from 2022), and 59.3% of facility residents had lived alone before admission (+22.0%p). **[V]** — [Segye 2026-08-06, 2025 장기요양실태조사](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260806512102)
- Japanese longitudinal research on about 26,000 people aged 65+ found that widowed men's quality of life worsened (higher dementia, mortality and ADL decline, with the first year riskiest), while widowed women's life satisfaction recovered over time. **[S]** — [Daum 2026-03-16](https://v.daum.net/v/20260316150307308)

### Inferences
- The segment is large and growing in absolute terms (about 1.8M → 4M households by household-head basis), but it is a **temporary life stage**. Each couple household eventually turns into a (mostly female) solo household. That makes "preparing for being left alone" a real need of the couple stage, not only of the solo stage.
- The falling share of couple households among the 65+ in 2020→2023 reflects both widowhood and the collapse of living with children.

### Gaps
- An exact 2052 count of 65+ couple households and its split by householder age group (KOSIS table). I relied on snippets and calculation.
- 2020 Census 65+ marital status by sex (share widowed among women and men). Search found no figure and KOSIS was unreachable.
- Average age at widowhood and the average length of widowhood for Korean women. No reliable source found.

## 2. Spousal caregiving (노노케어 / 배우자 돌봄 부담) and health management for two

### Takeaway
Families still provide most elderly care (81.4% of functionally limited older adults with a caregiver name family, 2023). The spouse is the heaviest-burdened family caregiver: older, poorer, usually a wife caring for a husband, and least supported. In the 2022 장기요양 data the spouse was the main family caregiver in **about 19%** of home-care cases, but over-represented among high-burden caregivers. Gender asymmetry is stark: men expect their wife to care for them, but women do not expect the same from their husbands.

### Cited Findings
- **2023 노인실태조사:** 18.6% of older adults have ADL/IADL limitations, and 47.2% of them receive care. Caregivers named: **가족 81.4%**, 장기요양 30.7% (up from 19.1% in 2020), 친척·이웃 20.0%, 개인 간병인 11.0%. Only 49.4% call their care sufficient. Top improvement wishes are rehabilitation/function training (37.0%), more varied service content (25.7%) and longer hours (24.0%). 6.6% have nobody to help in a crisis, rising to 12.9% among those 85+. **[V]** — [press release](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156655081)
- **2022 장기요양실태조사** (secondary analysis of home-care family caregivers, n≈2,048): caregiver relationship was **배우자 19.24%, 자녀 65.33%**, 며느리 and others the rest. In the latent-profile analysis, the **high-burden group had the highest spouse share (≈23.8% compared with 16.6–20.8% in other groups)**. That group was mostly women caring for male recipients, oldest, with the frailest recipients and the lowest income. The authors recommend stronger caregiver support services and paid leave during 가족돌봄휴가. **[V]** — [Kim·Lee·Song, 보건사회연구 44(1), 2024](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/hswr/assets/pdf/1463/journal-44-1-426.pdf)
- An earlier comparison of 321 spouse and 324 child caregivers found that spouses spend more time on ADL help and report higher overall, worry/strain and financial burden than child caregivers. **[S]** — [KoreaScience 2011](https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO201117148818037.pdf)
- KIHASA analysis of the 2017 노인실태조사: **63.2% of 65+ respondents who were caregiving were caring for a spouse**, versus 39.3% for a parent and 7.7% for a child. **[S]** — [KIHASA journal 38-2](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/hswr/assets/pdf/57/journal-38-2-257.pdf) (snippet attribution; not opened)
- **Gendered expectations** ("돌봄과 미래" demand survey of people 40+, 2025): when they need care, 49% of men expect their spouse to provide it, compared with 22% of women. Only about 4% expect it from children. **[S]** — [Newsis 2025-05-20](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20250520_0003183201), [Segye 2025-06-12](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20250612503968)
- **2025 장기요양실태조사** (released 2026-08-06; n=4,500 recipients plus families): 51.6% of families still feel care burden, although 84.6% are satisfied with the system. 43.5% of home-care families intend to keep caring at home even if health worsens (29.5% in 2022). 69.4% of home-care recipients want to stay at home (49.8% in 2022). Recipients average 3.34 chronic diseases (고혈압 65.0%, 치매 51.2%, 당뇨 35.4%). **[V]** — [Segye 2026-08-06](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260806512102)
- 가족인 요양 (a family member paid as the 요양보호사) rose from 4.7% (2008) to 15.9% (2019) of new home-care users. Use is highest when wives care for low-income husbands. **[S]** — [보건사회연구 46(3)](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/hswr/assets/pdf/1705/journal-46-3-145.pdf), [Supple](https://supple.kr/news/cmw6x2udzsyh7al9povurmwa2)
- Health baseline: 65+ adults average 2.2 chronic diseases, and 35.9% have 3 or more (2023). Couples average 2.0. **[V]** — press release
- **Policy/service landscape:** 장기요양 가족휴가제 is expanded to 12 days a year for severe and dementia recipients' families (단기보호 11→12일; 종일방문요양 22→24회) in the 2026 improvements. **[S]** — [KIHASA press 2026-11 (보건복지포럼 361호)](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/news/press/view?seq=77822). The 「의료·요양 등 지역 돌봄의 통합지원에 관한 법률」 takes effect in March 2026. 재택의료센터 go from 95 (2024) to 250 by 2027. 치매관리주치의 pilot covers 22 시군구 from July 2024. **[V]** — 2023 노인실태조사 press release
- **Japan reference (老老介護):** in the 2022 国民生活基礎調査, the spouse is the most common main caregiver living with the person (22.9% of all main caregivers; 子 16.2%). Among co-resident pairs of care recipient and main caregiver, **60+ both = 77.1%, 65+ both = 63.5%, 75+ both = 35.7%**, all rising. Among co-resident main caregivers providing "ほとんど終日" care, **女の配偶者 is 45.7%** (女の子 18.5%, 男の配偶者 15.7%). **[V]** — [MHLW 2022 国民生活基礎調査 概況 IV 介護](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/toukei/saikin/hw/k-tyosa/k-tyosa22/dl/05.pdf)

### Inferences
- The typical Korean 노노케어 case is a 70s–80s wife caring full-time for a husband with dementia or after a stroke, with low income, no paid work and no respite. Japan shows where this heads: 63.5% of co-resident care pairs are both 65+.
- Health management for two is a real joint need: two people's medications, appointments and chronic-disease monitoring, with the healthier spouse acting as coordinator until they can't. Couples' better self-rated health (48.6%) hides how fragile it is when one partner declines.
- Unmet needs: respite (only 12 days a year), skills training for spouse caregivers, home rehabilitation (37.0% top wish), and protecting the caregiver's own health.

### Gaps
- The share of spouses as main caregiver in the 2025 장기요양실태조사, and spouse-caregiver hours. These are in the 보건복지포럼 2026-11 issue, but the article PDF was not obtained.
- 2023 노인실태조사 tables on support exchanged with a spouse (report ch.6 §1, p.301) and the share of caregiving older adults who care for a spouse. The report PDF was not downloadable.
- 치매가족 지원 (치매안심센터 가족교실/쉼터) usage figures were not researched due to the tool budget.

## 3. Couple relationship, retired-husband conflict and 황혼이혼 risk

### Takeaway
Most older couples are satisfied: 70.3% of 65+ adults are satisfied with their spouse relationship (2024). There is a persistent **11.6%p gap between husbands (75.5%) and wives (63.9%)**. Long-marriage divorce is at a record high: marriages of 30+ years made up 17.7% of all 2025 divorces and for the first time outnumbered divorces within 4 years of marriage. Men 60+ are now about half of male divorce-counselling clients. The usual trigger is everyday friction after retirement (meals, housework, time together), helped along by divorce becoming more acceptable and by pension and property splitting.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 고령자 통계** (Statistics Korea, reference year 2024): **70.3%** of 65+ adults are satisfied with their spouse relationship (+5.3%p in two years), **husbands 75.5% compared with wives 63.9%**. 37.2% of 65+ couples are satisfied with the division of housework and 13.0% dissatisfied (+6.7%p satisfaction over 5 years). 57.1% oppose "남자는 일, 여자는 가정". In 2024, divorces of men 65+ rose 8.0% and of women 13.2%, while remarriages of 65+ men rose 6.4% and of women 15.1%. **[V]** — [Daum 2025-10-10](https://v.daum.net/v/20251010081348020)
- **2026 고령자 통계** (reference year 2024): 49.8% of 65+ adults say "이혼해서는 안 된다", down from 68.9% in 2014. The drop is larger among women (−20.9%p) than men (−16.9%p). In 2025, divorces of men 65+ rose 10.0% and of women 9.8%, while total divorces fell 3.3%. Marriages of 20+ years made up 36.6% of all divorces. **[V]** — [Daum (고령자 통계 2026)](https://v.daum.net/v/tKJPoHd8JO)
- **2025 혼인·이혼 통계:** there were **15,628** divorces after 30+ years of marriage, compared with 14,392 within 4 years (more for the first time since 1990, when the count was 368). They make up **17.7%** of 88,130 divorces, the largest duration group. **[V]** — [Segye 2026-05-19](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260519500959). Divorces where both spouses are 60+ numbered 13,743 (15.6%), a record. **[S]** — [Reportera](https://www.reportera.co.kr/news/twilight-divorce-surge-60s-couples-record-high-2025/?amp=1)
- **Drivers as reported (VOC):** long hours together after retirement, and the wife's resentment of being expected to cook and keep house ("이 정도는 당연히 해주겠지"). Divorce lawyers say clients "talk about daily-life stress before affairs". Pension and property splitting and the court's broad view of contribution make leaving financially feasible. In older divorces, property, housing, pension and living costs are the main issues, not custody. **[V]** — [Segye 2026-05-19](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260519500959)
- **한국가정법률상담소 2025 statistics:** divorce counselling was 5,090 cases, 24.7% of face-to-face counselling. Among male clients, **60+ = 49.1%** (12.5% in 2005). Among female clients, 60+ = 22.1% (5.8% in 2005). Women's main reason is 남편의 부당대우(폭력), 55.1%. Men's main reason is "기타 중대한 사유" (56.7%: long separation, personality, money, wife's demand for divorce). **[V]** — [Daum 2026-02-11](https://v.daum.net/v/20260211202236889)
- **은퇴남편증후군 / 삼식이:** the concept comes from Japan (主人在宅ストレス症候群, named in the 1990s): the wife's stress from a husband who is home all day. Men who lost their work identity tend to interfere at home. Advice is to keep separate time and space. **[S]** — [Seoul Shinmun 2009](https://m.seoul.co.kr/news/2009/02/21/20090221021003), [Economist.co.kr](https://economist.co.kr/article/view/ecn201708060025)
- **Emotional dependence is asymmetric** (Munhwa Ilbo/Embrain, baby boomers born 1955–63, n=1,000, Oct 2025): 59.6% name their spouse as their main emotional support (**men 72.2% compared with women 47.6%**). 75.4% of married respondents are satisfied with the spouse relationship. **[V]** — [Daum 2025-10-30](https://v.daum.net/v/20251030092146251)
- Alternatives to divorce: 졸혼 (separate lives while staying married) and "keeping distance" are rising themes. **[S]** — [Daum 2025-12-06](https://v.daum.net/v/20251206153300162)

### Inferences
- The core relationship need is **renegotiating roles after retirement**: housework, meals, personal time and space, and money. Husbands depend on the marriage far more than wives (72% compared with 48% emotional dependence; 75.5% compared with 63.9% satisfaction). Products and programs that make husbands self-sufficient in the home also reduce divorce risk and prepare for widowhood, so one intervention addresses two needs.
- Couple counselling or "retirement transition" coaching aimed at the 55–70 transition is underserved. Most counselling demand surfaces only at the divorce stage.

### Gaps
- No Korean national survey found on demand for 부부상담 among older couples, or on how many use 가족센터 couple programs.
- 2023 노인실태조사 spouse-relationship satisfaction (report ch.5 §3). Snippets show 66.9% or 69.4%, which conflict, so these are unverified.

## 4. Preparing for widowhood (사별 대비: housework skills for husbands, finances, 사후 정리)

### Takeaway
Widowed men are the most vulnerable: worse health, smaller networks and less contact from children. Most older husbands cannot feed themselves: 74.1% rely on meals cooked by their spouse. Values are shifting toward "spend on ourselves" (24.2% plan to use their assets for themselves and their spouse). A large planning gap remains, though: 86.6% of pension members don't know their expected pension, and only 1.6% have used retirement-planning services.

### Cited Findings
- Older men's cooking dependence (Dongguk Univ., Seoul/Gyeonggi, n=473 aged 60+, Oct–Nov 2020): **74.1% of older men eat meals prepared by their spouse; only 19.2% cook for themselves** (women 93.8% cook). Men who cook use semi-prepared food far more (31.1% compared with 8.9%). 47.0% of men eat out 4+ times a month. **[V]** — [Daum/Hankook 2021-08-18](https://v.daum.net/v/20210818194809917)
- Kyung Hee Univ. Digital New Aging Institute analysis of the 2020 노인실태조사: widowed men living alone have far worse physical and mental health than married men. They have **1.65 close relatives compared with 3.7**, under 3 close friends compared with about 5, and fewer children in weekly contact (57.6% compared with 63%). 13% hear from children only about once a quarter. **[V]** — [Daum (Q2nIcRdYzf)](https://v.daum.net/v/Q2nIcRdYzf)
- Local responses include 할아버지 요리교실 (완주 이서면 weekly class for men living alone; 단양군; 마포구 "꽃중년 남성 건강 요리교실" for men 40–70s). These target men who are already solo, not husbands preparing in advance. **[S]** — [Daum 2025-06-25](https://v.daum.net/v/20250625213443275), [Daum 4QdUl7SgtV](https://v.daum.net/v/4QdUl7SgtV), [Daum U7GV5B2uXF](https://v.daum.net/v/U7GV5B2uXF)
- Inheritance and asset intentions (2023 노인실태조사): "use for myself and spouse" went from **9.2% (2008) to 17.4% (2020) to 24.2% (2023)**. "Most to the eldest son" fell from 21.3% to 6.5%. Preferred funeral: 화장 후 납골당 38.0% and 자연장 23.1%; 19.6% have not thought about it. **[V]** — press release
- Retirement-planning gap (NPS 국민노후보장패널 10th supplementary survey, 2024; n=8,394 aged 50+ and spouses; released 2025-12-31): 86.6% of public-pension members don't know their expected benefit, and only **1.6%** have ever used a retirement-planning service. **[V]** — [Hankyung 2025-12-31](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202512315338Y)

### Inferences
- The concrete "preparing to be left alone" needs: (a) husbands' cooking, laundry and household-admin skills learned *while the wife is still alive*; (b) the wife learning household finances, insurance and digital banking if the husband handled them; (c) survivor-pension and asset structuring (유족연금 versus own pension choice, 주택연금 survivor continuation, wills and 상속); (d) a social network outside the marriage, especially for men.
- Japan shows women recover after widowhood while men decline. The service opportunity is therefore asymmetric: skill-building and community for husbands, and financial and administrative literacy for whichever spouse lacks it.

### Gaps
- No Korean survey found on what share of older couples have discussed or planned for widowhood (wills, 사전연명의료의향서 as a couple, survivor finances).
- No data on 유족연금 recipients by sex and age, or survivor-income drop after widowhood. Not searched due to budget.

## 5. Retirement income, housing (downsizing/aging in place), digital help

### Takeaway
Couples say they need about **2.98 million won a month** for an adequate life (2.17 million minimum). The average NPS benefit is about 670,000 won, so most couples rely on work (39.0% of older adults work), public transfers and housing wealth (97.0% own real estate, averaging 3.18억). They overwhelmingly want to stay at home (87.2% while healthy), but only 28.5% live in homes with features adapted for older people. That points to home modification and in-place care rather than relocation. Digital adaptation remains a pain point: 67.2% find it hard.

### Cited Findings
- **Adequate living cost** (NPS 국민노후보장패널 10th supplementary survey, 2024; released 2025-12-31): per individual, minimum 139.2만 and adequate 197.6만 won a month. **Per couple, minimum 216.6만 and adequate 298.1만 won.** The biggest spending item is food, followed by social insurance, health care and housing/utilities. Little change from the previous survey. **[V]** — [Hankyung 2025-12-31](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202512315338Y). (SBS Biz reported the couple figure as 296.9만, a discrepancy. **[S]**) The 2025 average NPS benefit was about 67만 won a month. **[S]** — [Reportera](https://www.reportera.co.kr/news/70s-couple-monthly-living-cost-retirement-budget/)
- **Income and assets of older adults (2023 노인실태조사):** annual household income 3,469만 won (2020: 3,027만). Sources: work and business 53.8%, public transfers 25.9%, private transfers 8.0%, property 6.7%. Financial assets 4,912만 won; real estate 3억1,817만 won; 97.0% own real estate. 39.0% of older adults work (2017: 30.9%). **[V]** — press release
- **Housing preferences (2023 노인실태조사):** 87.2% want to stay in their current home while healthy (2020: 83.8%). 48.9% want to stay even if they can no longer live independently. Only 28.5% live in homes with features adapted for older people; 62.1% live in homes that are not inconvenient but have no such features. **[S]** — search summary citing the report; [Hankyung 2025-02-17](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202502170706i)
- 2025 장기요양실태조사: the share of home-care recipients wanting to stay home rose to 69.4% (2022: 49.8%). Preference for nursing homes fell from 28.7% to 17.6%. **[V]** — [Segye 2026-08-06](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260806512102)
- **Rural relocation (2025 귀농어·귀촌인 통계, released 2026-06-25):** 귀농 household heads are 60대 37.3% and 50대 29.1%; 70+ farmers are at a record 8.5% (+17.3%). For 귀촌 household heads, 60대 = 15.5% and 70+ = 8.0%. The 60+ group was the only age group in which 귀촌 did not fall. **[S]** — [Hankyung 2026-06-25](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202606257539Y), [MAFRA](https://www.mafra.go.kr/bbs/home/792/578248/artclView.do)
- **Digital (2023 노인실태조사):** 67.2% find it hard to adapt to the information society. Smartphone ownership went from 56.4% to 76.6% (2020→2023), PC from 12.9% to 20.6%, and smartwatch is 2.1%. Wanted fixes: senior-tailored devices and apps (29.5%), more varied ICT education (27.4%) and device-fee support (21.9%). **[V]** — press release

### Inferences
- Couple-income needs center on turning housing wealth into income (주택연금, downsizing), coordinating two pensions, and part-time work. Downsizing appears to be a minority behavior. Stated preference strongly favors aging in place, so modifying the home *for two frail people* (bathroom, fall prevention, a separate rest space for the caregiver) is probably the larger housing need than relocation.
- Within a couple, digital competence is often held by one spouse. If that spouse dies or becomes disabled, the other loses access to banking, government apps and family chat. That is an under-discussed couple-specific digital risk. **[I]**

### Gaps
- No couple-specific income-adequacy data (share of couples below 298만) or 주택연금 statistics by household type. Not found within budget.
- No data on downsizing or relocation intentions by household type.

## 6. Shared leisure, travel, hobbies, community; relationship with adult children and grandchild care

### Takeaway
Older Koreans increasingly socialize through friendship groups rather than 경로당 (54.2% in 친목단체 compared with 26.5% using 경로당, 2023). Travel is the second most-wanted future leisure activity (44.6% overall, 56.7% at ages 65–69). Couple-specific travel data is thin: industry says retired-couple demand (cruises, premium packages) is rising but gave no figures. Grandchild care is a major drain on couples' time and health: grandparents average 6.04 hours a day and 26.8 hours a week, 53.3% do it involuntarily, and 51.1% are caring for a spouse at the same time.

### Cited Findings
- **Social participation (2023 노인실태조사):** 경로당 use 26.5% (2008: 46.9%). 친목단체 54.2% (2020: 44.1%). 동호회 6.6%. Volunteering 2.5% (falling). **[V]** — press release
- Wanted future leisure: **travel and tourism 44.6%** (2nd after 친목 46.2%). By age: 65–69 = 56.7%, 85+ = 19.4% (보건복지포럼 Nov 2024 reanalysis of 2023 노인실태조사). **[S]** — search summary; [KIHASA repository](https://repository.kihasa.re.kr/handle/201002/46065)
- Travel market: 50+ are 42.3% of domestic travelers (Korea Tourism Organization, cited for 2025). Travel and funeral-service (상조) firms are targeting cruises at retired couples, saying "은퇴 부부 여행 수요가 꾸준히 증가". 하나투어 has rebranded 5060 as "A Class". 5060 visits to performances and galleries are up, while visits to mountains and islands are down (관광데이터랩 2025). **[S]** — [MTN 2026-05-14](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026051414343839521), [Herald 2025-08-17](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2025081722360301603)
- **Relationship with children (2023 노인실태조사):** contact with children fell from 67.8% to 64.9%. 9.2% have no child they can contact (3.2% estranged, 6.0% no surviving child). Living with children fell to 10.3%. **[V]** — press release. Baby boomers' satisfaction with children and grandchildren is 84.8% (Munhwa/Embrain, Oct 2025). **[V]** — [Daum 2025-10-30](https://v.daum.net/v/20251030092146251)
- **Grandchild care (Korean Women's Development Institute 「노인의 손자녀 돌봄 실태조사」, released 2026-05-08):** on average **4.6 weekdays a week, 6.04 hours a day, 26.83 hours a week**. **53.3% involuntary** (women 57.5%, men 44.6%). **51.1% also care for a spouse or other family member** (women 56.4%). After starting, physical fatigue rose for 73.7%, mental stress for 60.4% and existing illness or pain for 47.8%. 46.8% have thought of quitting (women caring for 0–1-year-olds: 54.7%), mostly because it is physically too hard (46.7%). Positive side: relationship with grandchildren improved for 81.9% and with the adult child for 68.8%. **Relationship with spouse improved: men 48.4%, women 41.6%.** **[V]** — [Daum 2026-05-08](https://v.daum.net/v/20260508120153539)
- 2024 전국보육실태조사: only 36% of grandparent caregivers get regular cash, and 49.1% are effectively unpaid. 62% of Seoul dual-earner households get grandparent help (2024 서울시 양육자 실태조사). **[S]** — search summary (Kyeonggi 2025-09-09 and others: [Kyeonggi](https://kyeonggi.com/article/20250909580421))

### Inferences
- Couples' leisure needs come down to (a) affordable, low-exertion shared experiences (culture, short trips, cruises) at 65–74 while both are mobile; (b) **separate** activities and spaces for each spouse to ease retirement friction (from section 3); and (c) men-friendly community entry points, since 친목단체 are growing but men's networks are thin.
- Grandchild care is a "triple duty" for many couples: grandchildren, a spouse and their own health. The need is less for more childcare and more for boundaries, respite and health protection. **[I]**

### Gaps
- No hard data on the share of older couples who travel together, couple-travel spending or couple-product sales (Hanatour and Modetour IR not checked).
- 노인실태조사 travel experience rate by household type was not found.
- 부부 클래스 / joint learning program supply in 노인복지관 and 평생학습관 was not quantified.

## 7. Japan reference (brief)

### Takeaway
Japan is about 20 years ahead. Couple-only households are 43.6% of elderly households (2024). 老老介護 is the norm (63.5% of co-resident care pairs both 65+, 2022). Couple-targeted products have shifted from "couple" packaging to individual senior memberships.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 国民生活基礎調査: 2,760.4万 households include someone 65+ (50.3% of all households). Of these, 単独 32.7% and **夫婦のみ 31.8% (878.6万)**. Among 高齢者世帯 (all members 65+, or 65+ plus unmarried under-18s), 単独 52.5% and **夫婦のみ 43.6% (749.8万)**. Elderly singles are 64.0% women. **[V]** — [MHLW 2024 概況 (世帯数と世帯人員)](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/toukei/saikin/hw/k-tyosa/k-tyosa24/dl/02.pdf)
- 老老介護 (2022): spouse is the top co-resident main caregiver. 65+ both = 63.5%, 75+ both = 35.7%, both rising. Wives make up 45.7% of "almost all-day" caregivers. **[V]** — [MHLW 2022 概況 介護](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/toukei/saikin/hw/k-tyosa/k-tyosa22/dl/05.pdf)
- 主人在宅ストレス症候群 (the retired-husband stress syndrome) was named in Japan in the early 1990s and became Korea's 은퇴남편증후군. **[S]** — [Seoul Shinmun 2009](https://m.seoul.co.kr/news/2009/02/21/20090221021003)
- **JR フルムーン夫婦グリーンパス** (couples whose combined age is 88+; unlimited JR Green Car travel for 5, 7 or 12 days, e.g. 5 days from ¥77,800 in 2014) was not sold after Oct 2022 and is effectively discontinued. JR gave no official reason. Commentators point to the "couple" requirement fitting current lifestyles poorly, while 50+ memberships such as 大人の休日倶楽部 have become the norm. **[S]** — [tabiris](https://tabiris.com/archives/fullmoon2022/), [TravelVoice 2014](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20140828-26547)

### Inferences
- The discontinued フルムーン pass is a caution for Korea: products defined strictly by marital status may age badly as widowhood and divorce rise. Products that work for couples but are designed per person (with couple discounts or companion pricing) are more durable. **[I]**

### Gaps
- クラブツーリズム couple-tour data and current 大人の休日倶楽部 couple terms were not verified (www8.cao.go.jp was blocked; operator sites were not fetched).
