## 1. Size and growth of the segment (2024–2026 actuals; projections to 2035 and 2052)

### Takeaway
Korea had **8.045 million 1인가구 (36.1% of households) in 2024**, the first time above 8 million. The 2025 register census puts it at **8.24 million (36.6% of general households)**. Projections reach about 9.0 million (about 40%) by 2030, a peak of 9.95 million in 2043, and 9.62 million (41.3%) in 2052. Growth after 2030 is driven mainly by people aged 65 and over. The middle-aged slice is male-heavy: men are 33.3% of male 1인가구 at ages 40–59, against 21.6% for women. In Seoul, 80.5% of never-married people aged 40–59 already live alone.

### Inferences
- Within the 40–64 band, the counts available point to a sizeable never-married and divorced group, mostly male in the 40s–50s and growing at about 1 percentage point a year in Seoul. Nationally, 33.3% of male 1인가구 are aged 40–59; applied to roughly 3.9–4.0M male 1인가구, that suggests about 1.3M men aged 40–59 living alone in 2024. This is a rough estimate, not a published figure.
- The growth story after 2030 is mostly about older people. Today's 40–64 singles are the cohort that becomes the 65+ solo wave of the 2040s. Needs met in middle age (finance, health, networks, end-of-life planning) set up the older-age market.
- In Seoul, many middle-aged singles live alone by choice and have money: higher-income never-married people increasingly live alone. A separate low-income, isolated group sits alongside them. That points to two different need profiles (see section 5).

### Gaps
- No published 40–64 × sex breakdown of 1인가구 projections to 2035 or 2050 was found. It needs the KOSIS 장래가구추계 table (가구주 연령 × 가구원수), and kosis.kr was unreachable.
- A national split of 1인가구 by marital status (never married / divorced / widowed) for ages 40–64, 2024–2025, was not found. The 2023 가족실태조사 gives all ages only: 사별 37.8%, 미혼 37.2%, 이혼·별거 20.6%.
- The 2025 census 20% sample results (marital status × household type), due at the end of Nov 2026, were not yet available.

## 2. What they worry about and lack (survey evidence on needs)

### Takeaway
Across surveys, the same needs recur. Ranked by how often they appear and how severe they are:
1. **Coping alone when sick or in an emergency**, including the hospital 보호자 (guardian) problem.
2. **Money**: income, retirement funds, housing costs.
3. **Loneliness and having nobody to talk to.** This rises sharply from the 40s to the 60s and is worst among men and divorced people.
4. **Meals and nutrition**, especially for men.
5. **Housing stability**, the most-requested policy support.
6. **Safety and crime**, especially for women.
7. **End-of-life and post-death arrangements**, including pets.

### Cited Findings
**A. Sickness and emergencies (the top functional gap)**
- 통계로 보는 1인가구 2025 (social survey data from 2025). [FULL] — [Yonhap via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20251209120019970); [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20251209515305)
  - Have someone to help when sick: **68.9%**, against 75.1% for all households.
  - Have someone to help when depressed: 73.5%, against 78.8%.
  - Have someone to help when money is needed: 45.6%.
- 2023 가족실태조사 (여성가족부, released 2024-04-17). [FULL] — [Herald via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/Vuow9dEQ0F)
  - "아프거나 위급할 때 혼자 대처" (coping alone when sick or in an emergency) is a difficulty for **37.6%**, up from 30.9% in 2020.
  - Women 38.5%, men 36.0%.
- 서울시 1인가구 실태조사 (survey of 3,079 people in 2021, released 2022-05-10). [FULL] — [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/EuCbPssSUr)
  - **85.7%** report inconveniences of living alone.
  - The top inconvenience is coping when sick or in an emergency (**35.9%**), then meals (30.8%) and leisure (11.9%).
- Voice of customer, from KBS coverage of the first 고립·은둔 survey (2025-11): "병원 갔을 때 보호자 데리고 오세요 (하는데) '보호자 없습니다, 제가 보호자입니다'" (the hospital asks you to bring a guardian, and you answer that you have none and are your own guardian). [FULL] — [KBS via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20251111212947787)
- The guardian (보호자) problem:
  - Under 의료법 §24-2, an adult with decision-making capacity can sign their own surgery consent. 복지부 says no law or guideline requires a guardian to accompany a patient.
  - In practice many hospitals still ask for one, and one general hospital said it still takes a guardian's signature for surgery.
  - Posts asking how to find a hospital guardian recur on Naver cafés. People post on 당근 to hire an escort for sedated endoscopy (수면내시경 동행자).
  - [FULL] — [Yonhap fact-check via Daum 2026-04-24](https://v.daum.net/v/20260424080422499)
- Case of a never-married woman in her 50s with no siblings and parents deceased, diagnosed with a cerebral aneurysm, who has no one to sign a surgery consent. [FULL] — [Health Chosun via Daum 2025-04-22](https://v.daum.net/v/20250422083030345)
  - Hospitals still demand 연대보증-style (joint guarantor) "보호자 동의". 권익위 recommended a fix in 2017 that was not implemented.
  - 소비자정책위 (2019) recommended letting patients pre-designate a proxy. A 2022 의료법 amendment bill stalled.
  - Long-term cohabiting friends and 사실혼 (common-law) partners cannot consent.

### Inferences
- **Ranking of needs for middle-aged (40–64) singles.** This is my synthesis, weighting how often each need appears in surveys, its severity, and its specificity to this age group:
  1. **Help when sick or in an emergency, and a legal or practical substitute for the hospital 보호자.** Universal across surveys and rising (30.9% → 37.6%), with legal friction and visible demand for paid escorts (당근 postings).
  2. **Financial security and retirement funds.** Income is 46% of the average, rent is rising, 59% hold side jobs, and middle-aged men show large exclusion gaps on economy and employment.
  3. **Loneliness and a confidant.** This roughly doubles from the 40s (14.8%) to the 50s (24.5%). It is worst for men (31.3% have no confidant) and divorced people (38.6%), and the 40s never-married male has the lowest community belonging.
  4. **Isolation leading to 고독사.** Men in their 50s–60s are about 54% of cases; bodies are mostly found by landlords.
  5. **Meals and nutrition** (men 53%).
  6. **Housing stability** (37.9% top policy ask).
  7. **Safety** (strongest for women).
  8. **End-of-life planning and pets** (latent; services barely exist).
- **By sex.** Men need help with meals and daily self-care, need someone to talk to, and need to be found when isolated. Women need emergency and health help, safety, and economic independence and housing costs.
- **By income.** Loneliness and low satisfaction fall steeply with income. For high earners the unmet needs shift towards convenience, social connection and time, rather than material help.

### Gaps
- No **2024 or 2025 서울시 1인가구 실태조사** results were found. The most recent full Seoul survey located is the 2021 data released in 2022. The Seoul site pages that might hold newer results were unreachable.
- **2025 가족실태조사** (성평등가족부) results were not found. Only 2023 figures are used.
- No age-specific (40–64) breakdown of "help when sick" from the 2025 data was found.
- No Korean survey was found that directly measures how much middle-aged people worry about post-death arrangements (유품정리, 장례, 무연고 사망).

## 3. What public programs already cover, and where the gaps and complaints are

### Takeaway
Seoul's hospital-escort and loneliness-hotline programs show **strong demand that is disproportionately from middle-aged people**. 외로움안녕120 users are about 70–78% aged 40–64. But public escort is mostly used by people aged 65+ on dialysis, and it is capped and means-tested for free use. The structural gaps are:
- the legal hospital-guardian and surgery-consent problem,
- weak support for middle-aged men, whom one book's author calls the "weakest area" of social security,
- application-based programs that miss isolated people,
- no pre-designated proxy system,
- almost no end-of-life or pet-succession services.

### Cited Findings
- **Seoul 병원안심동행** (hospital escort, launched 2021-11). [FULL] — [FN via Daum 2024-10-20](https://v.daum.net/v/20241020111511044)
  - The escort manager accompanies the user door to door, including reception, payment and pharmacy.
  - Cumulative **45,001 uses** by Oct 2024, 29% above the 35,000 target.
  - Last 12 months: 19,037 uses (+5.7%). **62% were 1인가구; of those, 65+ 77% and middle-aged 21%.**
  - Purpose: appointments 48.6%, dialysis 29.8%, tests 8.4%.
  - Satisfaction **92.9%**.
- In 2022, 92.2% of escort users were aged 50+. [SNIPPET] — search summary of Seoul mediahub (unreachable)
- From Jan 2025, use rose from twice a week to 10 times a month. Free for people at or below 100% of median income, up to 48 times a year. Cumulative about 70,000 uses was also reported. [SNIPPET]
- 성평등가족부 planned to support hospital-escort services for 1인가구 nationally through family centers. [SNIPPET] — [Daum search result](https://v.daum.net/v/20240417170313481)
- **외로움안녕120** (loneliness hotline, launched 2025-04-01). [SNIPPET] — search summaries of [Seoul mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2017478) and Herald 2026-06-06, both unreachable for full reading
  - About **40,000 calls by end of Mar 2026** (about 125 a day). The Seoul Welfare Foundation interview cites about 33,000, so the counting basis is unclear.
  - **Users aged 40–60s: 77.6%.** A late-2025 briefing put people aged 40–64 at 71.1% of participants.
  - Middle-aged men use it more than women.
  - Common topics: family breakdown, business failure, conflict with children, bereavement.
- Seoul's '외로움 없는 서울' (외없서) project is to focus on the middle-aged in 2026. [SNIPPET] — headline from Herald 2025-12-17
- Other Seoul programs named alongside the 중년 미혼 report: 서울마음편의점, 외로움안녕120 and 365일 서울챌린지. The city admits current support "충분히 미치지 못하고 있다" (does not reach them enough) and calls for discussing a '사회적 가족' (social family) system. [FULL] — [Dong-A via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260507145953189)
- Seoul's 4th 고독사 prevention plan (2021). [FULL, but dated 2021] — [JoongAng via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/cRl3rZw7B6)
  - Special full survey of 1인가구 aged 50–64.
  - '서울 살피미' app alerts a guardian or the 주민센터 after 24 hours without phone use.
  - Smart plugs for 3,100 middle-aged at-risk households.
  - Seoul's official 2020 고독사 count was 51, but 667 무연고 (unclaimed) deaths were suspected.
- National policy:
  - From 2026, 고독사 policy expands to "사회적 고립 위험군" (people at risk of social isolation) by life stage. For people in their 50s–60s: job information and middle-aged self-help groups. A 사회적 고립 실태조사 (social isolation survey) is planned. [FULL] — [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/Y6Q0iaKpAe)
  - The government is reviewing raising 생계급여 (livelihood benefit) for 1인가구, since they carry housing costs alone. 복지부 1차관 (first vice-minister) is designated to lead on isolation and loneliness. [FULL] — [Edaily via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260115051644520)
  - 국회미래연구원 '1인가구 증가 대응 방안' recommends low-barrier neighbourhood community spaces for information, mutual aid and emotional support. [FULL, reported] — same Edaily article
- Gaps and complaints:
  - Hospitals still demand a guardian or 연대보증. Proxy pre-designation is not law: recommended in 2019, bill in 2022, no progress. [FULL] — [Health Chosun via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20250422083030345); [Yonhap via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260424080422499)
  - Ambulance crews report hospitals refusing patients who have no guardian. [SNIPPET] — [Daum 2025-10-31](https://v.daum.net/v/20251031113138412)
  - 간호·간병통합서비스 (nurse-provided integrated care wards, no family carer needed) exists, but articles call for its expansion. [FULL] — [Yonhap via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260424080422499)
  - "중장년을 대상으로 한 사회보장과 서비스 지원은 … 가장 취약한 영역" (social security and services for the middle-aged remain the weakest area). Application-based welfare fails isolated people; one example given is a middle-aged woman who was rejected for basic livelihood support and then attempted suicide. [FULL] — [Maeil book review via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260717171201077)
  - Policy has historically targeted 독거노인 (older people living alone). Middle-aged men slip through: a 주민센터 (community center) knows registered older people but not middle-aged singles registered with family. [FULL, older feature] — [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/LhtHRlhmiw)
  - Pet succession and disposal have no legal framework, and pet funeral access is thin. [FULL] — [Segye via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/HWP5rrb0oL)

### Inferences
- **Covered reasonably well:** hospital escort for low-income and older people (Seoul), a loneliness hotline, and digital check-in apps.
- **Thin or uncovered:**
  - escort and companionship for working-age, middle-income singles (free use is means-tested, and users skew 65+ and dialysis);
  - a legally recognised substitute guardian or proxy;
  - post-discharge recovery help;
  - meal solutions for men;
  - end-of-life planning (funeral, estate, pets, digital assets) for non-poor singles;
  - repairs, moving and daily-errand help. No survey data was found on this; it appears only as anecdote.
- 외로움안녕120's age mix (about 71–78% aged 40–64) is the strongest behavioural signal that middle-aged singles will use a "someone to talk to" service when it is free and anonymous.

### Gaps
- Full-text verification of the 외로움안녕120 statistics and the 2025–2026 escort statistics was not possible, because the Seoul mediahub and Herald hosts were blocked.
- The fee for non-free escort users and any complaint data (waiting times, booking failures) were not found.
- 마음편의점 and 안심귀가 (safe walk home) usage and complaint data were not found.

## 4. Voice of customer and willingness to pay (what they currently pay for)

### Takeaway
Direct evidence on willingness to pay is thin. The behavioural signals are:
- people hiring hospital escorts through 당근 postings;
- 59.6% working side jobs to cover costs;
- high rent burden;
- low use of paid pet funerals by 1인가구 (19.3%; the reason was not asked);
- price anxiety about funerals (a quoted average of about 2,000만원).

Free public services show high satisfaction: 92.9% for hospital escort.

### Cited Findings
- Monthly spending of a 1인가구 is 168.9만원: housing and utilities 18.4%, food and lodging 18.2%, groceries 13.6%. Health spending is 12.2만원/month (outpatient 41.7%, medicines 18.6%, inpatient 15.7%). [FULL] — [Yonhap via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20251209120019970)
- People post on 당근 to hire escorts for sedated endoscopy, and Naver café posts ask "1인 가구나 혼자인 분들은 어떻게 병원 치료 받으시나요?" (how do people living alone get hospital treatment?). [FULL] — [Yonhap via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260424080422499)
- KBS interviewee (a 1인가구 who went bankrupt through card debt): "가족이 있으면 이렇게 되지도 않았겠죠" (this would not have happened if I had family), and "명절날 외롭고. 아플 때" (lonely at the holidays, and when sick). [FULL] — [KBS via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20251111212947787)
- Pet funeral spending: 44.3% paid 20–50만원. 1인가구 rarely use pet funeral businesses (19.3%). [FULL] — [Segye via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/HWP5rrb0oL)
- KB 2026: 1인가구 are shifting money into stocks and ETFs (21.1% of financial assets) and taking investment loans (34.0% of borrowers). Demand for asset-management help is rising: "자산관리·재테크가 어렵다" is up 5.2 points. [FULL] — [Maeil via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260719103001551)
- Middle-aged caregiving burden: middle-aged people saying parent care should be shared by "family, government and society" rose from 49% to 65% over 10 years. About 70% of home-care recipients want to stay at home. Middle-aged singles are often the sole carer for their own parents. [FULL] — [Yonhap via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260923081554800)

### Inferences
- Paid demand is most visible for **hospital escort and companionship** (peer hiring on 당근), **financial and asset advice**, and probably **funeral and end-of-life planning**, given the price anxiety and DIY behaviour. Free public provision anchors price expectations low for low-income users. Middle-income working singles who do not qualify for free services are the probable paying segment.
- The middle-aged face a "double burden": living alone themselves and caring for parents alone. That suggests demand for parent-care coordination services.

### Gaps
- No Korean survey was found that quantifies willingness to pay (WTP in KRW) for escort, safety-check, meal or end-of-life services among people aged 40–64.
- No app-review mining was done (Play Store or App Store reviews of 1인가구 service apps), and community-forum data was not systematically sampled.

## 5. How needs differ by age, sex and income

### Takeaway
- **Age.** Loneliness and having no confidant grow sharply from the 40s to the 60s.
- **Sex.** Men are worse on meals, confidants, isolation and 고독사. Women are worse on emergencies, health and safety.
- **Income.** This is the strongest single divider of loneliness and life satisfaction.

### Cited Findings
- Loneliness as a difficulty of living alone, by age: 40s 14.8%, 50s 24.5%, 60s 30.2%. No confidant: 40s 20.6%, 50s 25.5%, 60s 30.8%. [FULL] — [Herald via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/Vuow9dEQ0F)
- Men: balanced meals hard 53.0% (women 36.3%); no confidant 31.3% (women 20.6%); 81.7% of 고독사. Women: coping in emergencies 38.5% (men 36.0%); violent-crime victimization 2.1% in Seoul. [FULL] — [Herald via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/Vuow9dEQ0F); [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/Y6Q0iaKpAe); [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/EuCbPssSUr)
- Divorced or separated people have the highest no-confidant rate, at 38.6%. [FULL] — [Herald via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/Vuow9dEQ0F)
- Never-married men in their 40s living alone have the lowest community belonging (3.0/10, Seoul). [FULL] — [Dong-A via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260507145953189)
- Income, Seoul never-married people aged 40–59 living alone: life satisfaction 5.5 (under 200만원) against 7.7 (800만원+); loneliness 4.5 against 2.4. Managerial and professional workers have the most active leisure (weekday 36.1%, weekend 47.1%). [FULL] — [Munhwa via Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20260508151715614)
- Middle-aged male single householders show far higher exclusion risk on economy and employment (+21.5 and +20.6 points) than male multi-person householders. [SNIPPET] — [KIHASA](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/hswr/assets/pdf/1693/journal-46-2-538.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Two distinct middle-aged single segments emerge.**
  - **(a) Affluent, voluntary, never-married professionals.** Most common in Seoul. Needs: convenience, health and emergency backup, community and belonging, asset and retirement planning, and end-of-life planning. They are able to pay.
  - **(b) Low-income, involuntary singles, often divorced men in their 50s–60s.** Needs: income and jobs, meals, mental health, being found and checked on, and 고독사 prevention. Mostly served by public programs.
- The **40s never-married man** (lowest belonging) and the **50s–60s divorced man** (highest 고독사 risk, no confidant) look like the least-served needs, with very different ability to pay.

### Gaps
- No published cross-tab of age × sex × marital status × "needs" from a single national survey (for example, divorced men aged 50–64) was found in full text.

## 6. Japan reference (brief)

### Takeaway
Japan is further along: about 44% of households are projected to be single by 2050. Japanese survey data likewise shows loneliness concentrated among people living alone and among never-married and divorced people, with men and women in their 50s living alone among the loneliest.

### Cited Findings
- 内閣府 「人々のつながりに関する基礎調査」 令和6年 (survey date 2024-12-01; 10,871 respondents aged 16+; published Apr 2025). [FULL] — [cao.go.jp PDF](https://www.cao.go.jp/kodoku_koritsu/torikumi/zenkokuchousa/r6/pdf/tyosakekka_gaiyo.pdf)
  - Overall: "しばしばある・常にある" (often or always lonely) 4.3%, 時々 (sometimes) 15.4%, たまに (occasionally) 19.6%.
  - Living alone: **9.0%** often or always lonely, against 3.5% for people living with others.
  - Living alone, ages 40–49: 9.8% often or always, plus 21.6% sometimes.
  - Living alone, ages 50–59: **11.9%** often or always, plus 22.9% sometimes.
  - For both men and women, loneliness is higher among the 未婚 (never married) and 離別 (divorced).
  - The age figures are read from table layout text, so there is a small risk of column misalignment.
- 社人研 (IPSS) 2024 household projection: single households will be **44.3% of all households in 2050**. Among households headed by someone aged 65+, the single share rises from 35.2% (2020) to 45.1% (2050). [SNIPPET] — [Dai-ichi Life Research PDF](https://www.dlri.co.jp/files/ld/556150.pdf)
- 令和7年版高齢社会白書: by 2050, the share of people aged 65+ living alone reaches 26.1% for men and 29.3% for women. [SNIPPET]
- An older study (ニッセイ基礎研究所, 2009) found middle-aged unmarried people more likely than married people to expect "頼れる人がいなくなる" (having nobody to rely on) and "老後生活費が不足" (not enough money for retirement). [SNIPPET, dated] — [NLI PDF](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/topics/38210_ext_18_0.pdf?site=nli)

### Inferences
- Japan's pattern of loneliness concentrated among people living alone, never married and divorced, peaking in the 50s, mirrors Korea's 가족실태조사 and 고독사 data. Japanese service categories that address the same needs are a reasonable analogue for the needs listed above: 身元保証 (identity and guarantor services), 見守り (watch-over), 終活 (end-of-life planning) and 死後事務委任 (contracts for post-death affairs).

### Gaps
- Japanese surveys of おひとりさま service needs (身元保証, 終活, 見守り) and specific service examples were not researched in this pass. The sibling folder `research_notes/일본 시니어 솔로 서비스 사례/` likely covers them.
- The 令和7年 (2025) 内閣府 loneliness survey results were not located.
