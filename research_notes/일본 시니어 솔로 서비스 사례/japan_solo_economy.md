# Japan's Solo Economy (ソロ経済・ソロ活・おひとりさま市場) for single, never-married and divorced people aged 30–64: market size, Japanese cases, and what transfers to Korea (as of Nov 2026)

> **How this was gathered:** ~29 web searches, run 2026-11-19. **Most figures below come from search-result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads.** The one full-page fetch I tried (businessinsider.jp) was blocked by the session's network settings (`www.businessinsider.jp` has to be allowed). Treat every number as "snippet-level". Check it against the linked primary source before you quote it externally. Dates are the publication or reference date of each figure.

## 1. Market sizing: solo-economy estimates, single-household consumption, lifetime-unmarried trends

### Takeaway
No credible yen figure for the "solo economy" turned up. That includes 荒川和久's estimate, which I could not find in the searchable sources. What is solid is the demographic base:
- 50歳時未婚率 (2020): men 28.25%, women 17.81%.
- Single-person households: 38.0% in 2020, projected 44.3% by 2050.
- Single people overall: about 47% of the population by 2040.

### Cited Findings
**Lifetime unmarried rate (生涯未婚率 / 50歳時未婚割合)**
- 50歳時未婚割合, 2020 国勢調査: men 28.25%, women 17.81%. This is 社人研's 人口統計資料集 figure, computed on the 不詳補完値 (imputed) basis. — [IPSS 人口統計資料集 2026 表6-23](https://www.ipss.go.jp/syoushika/tohkei/Data/Popular2026/T06-23.files/sheet001.htm)
- The comparable 2015 figures were men 24.77% and women 14.89%. — [生命保険文化センター](https://www.jili.or.jp/lifeplan/lifeevent/805.html)
- **Method caveat:** on the traditional basis (excluding 配偶関係不詳), 2020 is men 25.7% and women 16.4%.
  - 総務省統計局 told Newsweek Japan that 不詳補完値 are reference values and not appropriate for comparison with past years.
  - Source: [Newsweek Japan](https://www.newsweekjapan.jp/articles/-/45948)
- 社人研's 2018 projection had put 2020 at men 26.7% and women 17.5%, so actual 2020 came in above it. The 2015-based projection has men reaching about 29.5% in 2040. — [Newsweek Japan](https://www.newsweekjapan.jp/articles/-/45948); [IPSS WPJ35](https://ipss.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/56/files/IPSS_WPJ35.pdf)

**Single-person households (単独世帯)**
- 社人研「日本の世帯数の将来推計（全国推計）」令和6（2024）年推計:
  - 単独世帯 share rises from 38.0% (2020) to 44.3% (2050).
  - That is about 23.3 million single households in 2050, out of about 52.61 million total households.
  - Single-person households aged 65+ reach about 10.84 million in 2050, roughly 1.5× 2020.
  - Sources: [厚労省資料](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12600000/001334405.pdf); [nippon.com Japan Data](https://www.nippon.com/ja/japan-data/h02201/)
- The number of single households is described as peaking around 2035 at about 24.5 million (per LIFULL HOME'S's reading of the 社人研 projection). Average household size falls to 1.92 by 2050. — [LIFULL HOME'S 不動産投資](https://toushi.homes.co.jp/column/?p=15685); [EN-ICHI / IPP](https://ippjapan.org/en_ichi/en/archives/498)

**Single people as a share of the population (荒川和久 / ソロ社会)**
- 社人研's 2018 estimate: in 2040, married people are 53% and single people (never-married, divorced or widowed) are 47%.
  - The popular "独身5割" line is a rounding of this 47%.
  - 荒川和久 (author of 『超ソロ社会』, leader of 博報堂ソロもんLABO) uses this figure.
  - Sources: [GetNavi book review](https://getnavi.jp/book/728839/); [野村證券 Real80s interview](https://www.nomura.co.jp/wealthstyle/real80s/0011/)
- 荒川's 東洋経済 column says single-person households will be about 40% of households by 2040. This is a different metric from the share of single people. — [東洋経済オンライン](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/290175?display=b)

**博報堂 research**
- 博報堂's 「ソロ活動系男子（ソロ男）」 project (2014) targeted single men aged 20–50s.
  - It found lower average income than married men, but higher disposable income and stronger consumption appetite.
  - Source: [トラベルボイス 2014-09](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20140918-27416/print)
- 博報堂生活総合研究所 published 「ひとりマグマ」 research in 2024. It focuses on "solo" consumption, including people who live with family but buy things for themselves. — [ITmedia 2025-03](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/business/articles/2503/12/news083.html)
- 博報堂生活総研「シニア40年変化」 (2026-07), ages 60–74:
  - 66.4% say they act alone more than with friends.
  - About 40% express a 卒婚 (amicable separation within marriage) intention.
  - Sources: [生活総研 News Release 2026-07-01](https://seikatsusoken.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/20260701_-NewsRelease_seikatsusoken.pdf); [Web担当者Forum / PR TIMES](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/r/prtimes/items/000001188.000008062)

**Single-household consumption**
- No national figure turned up for the share of total consumption spent by single households. A regional data point:
  - 愛媛県 summary of 2024 data: single-household 消費支出 of ¥169,223/month.
  - 埼玉県 summary: single working households' 実収入 is ¥323,191/month and 可処分所得 is ¥268,696/month.
  - Sources: [愛媛県](https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/attachment/169326.pdf); [埼玉県](https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/documents/256314/r6zenkokukakei_kekka_.pdf)
- ニッセイ基礎研究所 analyses single working households by age bracket (under 35, and 35–59); I did not get its numbers. — [NLI Research](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/topics/83011_ext_18_0.pdf?site=nli)

### Inferences
- The 30–64 single segment is structurally large and still growing:
  - Men's lifetime unmarried rate is heading toward about 30%.
  - About 44% of households will be single by 2050.
  - Most single households in the 2030s will be middle-aged and older, not young.
- Japanese "solo economy" talk is mostly qualitative. The often-quoted "100兆円" figure is unsourced, so do not cite it.

### Gaps
- 荒川和久's own ソロエコノミー yen estimate. Not found in search, and his books are not online.
- 野村総研 / 三菱総研 solo-market estimates. None found.
- 家計調査 2024 national table of single working households by age (34歳以下 / 35〜59歳 消費支出). I did not get the actual figures. Use [総務省統計局 家計調査](https://www.stat.go.jp/data/kakei/2024np/pdf/summary.pdf) directly.

## 2. Cases by category (company, model, price, users, revenue, growth)

### Takeaway
Two clear groups emerge:
- **Businesses with public scale data:**
  - nosh (150M meals cumulative)
  - 三ツ星ファーム (40M meals)
  - IBJ (¥20.1B revenue, 20,970 marriages in 2025)
  - マリッシュ (about 4M users)
  - クラブツーリズム ひとり旅 (record sales)
  - 自治体 + イントラスト 養育費保証 (spreading by municipality)
- **Small, founder-run or niche:** おっさんレンタル, レンタル家族, ソロウェディング. These have little or no revenue data.

### 2-1. ソロ活 / ソロエンタメ
- **焼肉ライク (ダイニングイノベーション, unlisted)**
  - Format: one-person grill counters.
  - About 72 stores, per an Aug 2026 press release.
  - Launched a 「一人焼肉食べ放題」 in some stores from 2026-04-20, expanding a Dec 2025 pilot.
  - Revenue is not disclosed.
  - Sources: [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000475.000034691.html); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000476.000034691.html)
- **Context for the yakiniku segment**
  - 2025 yakiniku bankruptcies hit a record 59 (+31.1%, 東京商工リサーチ). Another count gives 57 for FY2025.
  - 牛角 has about 480 domestic stores (Apr 2026) and is described as shrinking. — [現代ビジネス](https://gendai.media/articles/-/164286)
  - *Inference:* solo-format chains are growing against a weak overall yakiniku market.
- **ワンカラ (コシダカHD)**
  - Opened Nov 2011 in 神田 as the first solo-only karaoke format.
  - The idea came from about 20% of ordinary karaoke customers already visiting alone.
  - The article cites 6 stores, an old figure.
  - Source: [All About](https://allabout.co.jp/gm/gc/452319/)
  - No current store count found.
- **クラブツーリズム 「おひとり参加限定」 tours (solo-only group tours)**
  - 2014: about 1,500 courses a year and about 35,000 participants. — [トラベルボイス 2014-02](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20140209-16501)
  - FY2023: over 1,300 domestic solo courses, sales up 33% year on year to a record. Women in their 30s–50s are increasing on top of the core 60–70s. — [トラベルボイス 2024-06](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20240604-155731)
  - 2013 survey: the most common age for a first solo trip was the 50s, and 67% made their first solo trip at 50 or later. — [トラベルボイス 2013-05](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20130503-4705)
- **ソロキャンプ**
  - オートキャンプ白書2023: the solo-camp share rose from 13.1% (2021) to 16.6% (2022). Auto-camping population was 6.5M in 2022 (86.7% of the prior year). — [DIME](https://dime.jp/genre/1308036/); [北海道新聞](https://www.hokkaido-np.co.jp/article/888001/)
  - 白書2025 (covering 2024) reports the average number of trips fell, at 5.0 a year. No 2024 solo share found. — [日本オートキャンプ協会 PDF](https://www.autocamp.or.jp/2020/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/e2e423b946154eebea89bed69c38ea27.pdf)
  - *Inference:* this is a post-COVID boom that is now plateauing, i.e. partly a fad.
- **ソロウェディング (solo wedding photo plans)**
  - Mostly photo plans.
  - Example: プリンスホテル鎌倉 at ¥138,000+ per person (2026-10-22 to 2027-03-31). Includes venue, one dress and photos; weekends add ¥38,500. — [Prince Hotels](https://www.princehotels.co.jp/kamakura/wedding/informations/202610_weddingphotoplan/)
  - English-language coverage frames it as a product for single women. — [Unseen Japan](https://unseen-japan.com/solo-weddings-japan-single-women/)
  - No user or revenue data. Niche.

### 2-2. 住まい (housing)
- **シェアハウス (オークハウス etc.)**
  - オークハウス is described as one of Japan's largest share-house operators; its minimum age is 18+. — [ieagent](https://ieagent.jp/blog/life/sharehouse-age-344375)
  - Industry-wide, residents aged 40+ are increasing, and some properties house people in their 80s. — [livedoor news](https://news.livedoor.com/article/detail/31321392/)
  - Business Insider Japan (Feb 2026) covered why people in their 30s choose expensive share houses. It was blocked, so I have no numbers from it. — [BI Japan](https://www.businessinsider.jp/article/2602-why-30s-choose-expensive-share-houses/)
- **ADDress (multi-base living subscription)**
  - About 300 bases in Japan and abroad (Apr 2026). Raised funding from ANA未来ファンド. — [THE BRIDGE 2026-04](https://thebridge.jp/2026/04/address-raises-funding-from-ana-mirai-fund)
  - Early pricing (c. 2019–20): ¥40,000/month on an annual plan, ¥45,000 on a half-year plan, ¥50,000 on a trial month. — [ITmedia 2019-12](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/business/spv/1912/25/news035.html)
  - Plan changes:
    - Jan 2023: a ¥9,800 plan for 2 nights a month.
    - Nov 2023: a ¥980/month community-only plan (events, no stays).
    - Over 40% of members are company employees.
    - Source: [トラベルボイス 2023-12](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20231204-154679)
  - No member count published.
  - *Inference:* cutting price tiers this hard suggests the core full-time-nomad demand was limited.
- **シングルマザー住宅 (LivEQuality大家さん)**
  - Japan-style affordable housing for single mothers. Announced fundraising in Feb/Mar 2026, linked to a Tokyo public-private fund. — [創業手帳 2026-03](https://sogyotecho.jp/news/20260303livequality/)

### 2-3. 食・家事 (food and housework)
- **nosh (ナッシュ, frozen low-carb meal subscription)**
  - Cumulative meals sold: 120M (Mar 2025), 130M (Jun 2025), 140M (Sep 2025), 150M (Dec 2025). That is about 10M meals per quarter.
  - Produces 100,000 meals a day at its own 尼崎 factory.
  - Revenue was not in the results.
  - Sources: [LOGI-TODAY](https://www.logi-today.com/905867); [digitalpr](https://digitalpr.jp/r/127122)
- **三ツ星ファーム (イングリウッド, frozen meal subscription)**
  - Cumulative shipments: 35M meals (Sep 2025), 40M (end of Jan 2026).
  - Cumulative members: 300k+ as of Sep 2025. Another headline in the same article says 380k, so the figures conflict.
  - Pivoted in Nov 2025 from a plate subscription to a general food e-commerce store.
  - Parent イングリウッド's revenue was ¥24.2B for FY Aug 2024. That is the whole group, not 三ツ星 alone.
  - Sources: [ECのミカタ](https://ecnomikata.com/original_news/50666/); [Business Journal](https://biz-journal.jp/company/post_386439.html)
- **家事代行 (CaSy, タスカジ)**
  - Market: 矢野経済 put it at ¥98B (FY2012) in one report and ¥81.8B (2013) with ¥90.6B forecast for 2017 in another. The two series conflict, and both are old. — [矢野経済](https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/4191); [J-Net21](https://j-net21.smrj.go.jp/startup/guide/service/service39.html)
  - タスカジ (Dec 2015): 2,900+ clients and 170+ housekeepers 1 year 5 months after launch. — [THE BRIDGE](https://thebridge.jp/2015/12/taskaji-end-of-the-year-campaign)
  - CaSy (listed on TSE Growth, code 9215): about 97% of revenue comes from housekeeping. — [Holistic Research](https://holistic-r.org/?p=6405)
  - Current figures not retrieved.

### 2-4. つながり / コミュニティ (connection)
- **おっさんレンタル (rent a middle-aged man)**
  - ¥1,000/hour, 13 years in operation.
  - The founder says about 30 orders a day. About 80% of users are women in their 30s–40s, with uses ranging from advice to tourist guiding.
  - Source: [President Online](https://president.jp/articles/-/97521)
- **レンタル家族 (ファミリーロマンス)**
  - Cited at a few thousand to about ¥10,000 per hour; the date is unclear.
  - The first rental-family service in Japan was 日本能率厚生本部 in 1991.
  - Sources: [文春オンライン](https://bunshun.jp/articles/-/58773); [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rent_a_family)
  - No revenue data.
- **Adult friend-making apps and 社会人サークル:** no Japanese case with numbers turned up (see Gaps).

### 2-5. 婚活 / 再婚 (matchmaking and remarriage)
- **IBJ (TSE Prime 6071, marriage-agency platform)**
  - FY Dec 2025: revenue ¥20.1B, operating profit ¥3.6B, 20,970 marriages (成婚組数).
  - 2027 targets raised to ¥31.5B revenue, ¥4.8B operating profit and 30,000 marriages.
  - Members: 97,857 registered and 77,900 paying (Jun 2025). End-2024 had 70,697 paying.
  - The way marriages are counted changed in 2025.
  - Sources: [IBJ IR PDF 2026-02](https://www.ibjapan.jp/information/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/b3a1cea67ca7f609b7412d8df46c72b1.pdf); [JPX 適時開示](https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/60710/140120250701506200.pdf)
- **マリッシュ (marrish, remarriage and single-parent app)**
  - About 4M users from its 2016 launch to Jun 2025 (company media guide; unclear whether this is cumulative registrations).
  - "Over 60% have a divorce history," per third-party media.
  - Its リボンマーク feature lets members show they welcome partners with children (子連れ婚活).
  - Sources: [marrish media guide](https://speakerdeck.com/marrish/marrish-media-guide); [ekitan](https://ekitan.com/media/marriage/?p=18112)

### 2-6. 離婚関連 (divorce-related)
- **養育費保証 (child-support guarantee), イントラスト + 東京海上日動**
  - Model: if the payer defaults, イントラスト pays the recipient directly, then collects from the payer and claims unrecovered amounts from 東京海上日動 insurance.
  - Municipal rollouts:
    - 鹿児島県出水市, 香川県三豊市 and 兵庫県加古川市 from 2026-04-01.
    - 高松市 from 2026-10-01.
    - **All 27 municipalities of 山梨県 from 2026-08-20**, the first prefecture-wide rollout.
  - Sources: [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000015.000138581.html); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000021.000138581.html); [ReseMom 2026-04-28](https://resemom.jp/release/prtimes/20260428/181392.html)
- **Municipal subsidies**
  - 羽曳野市 (from 2025-06-02): the guarantor advances up to ¥50,000/month if support goes unpaid, and the city pays the first-year guarantee fee. — [羽曳野市](https://www.city.habikino.lg.jp/material/files/group/90/20250528_houdouteikyou.pdf)
  - 長野県 subsidises 公正証書 (notarised agreement) costs plus guarantee contract fees for single parents in towns and villages. — [長野県](https://www.pref.nagano.lg.jp/kodomo-katei/hitorioya/yoikuhi.html)
- **リライフテクノロジー 「リコ活」 (divorce-support platform)**
  - Uses a 課題発見型カルテ (problem-diagnosis chart) and matches users with lawyers and couples counsellors, through to "再出発". Raised funding in Jan 2025. — [創業手帳 2025-01](https://sogyotecho.jp/news/20250110relife-technology)
- **「ふたり会議」 (couple-conversation tech):** framed around "one divorce every 3 minutes". — [Kepple](https://kepple.co.jp/articles/h4n0rtmaxv6)

### 2-7. 将来不安 (future anxieties)
- **身元保証・高齢者等終身サポート事業 (guarantor and lifelong support for people without family)**
  - 総務省 had identified 398 operators as of Aug 2023, plus 12 more from local-government interviews. 厚労省 says the count grew to 400+ on the back of 孤独死 fears and the 終活 boom.
  - Weaknesses: no regulator, no entry barrier, and a tendency to rely on donation income.
  - Sources: [総務省](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000895034.pdf); [厚労省](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12602000/001756938.pdf)
  - Cross-ministry 「高齢者等終身サポート事業者ガイドライン」 issued Jun 2024 by 9 ministries and agencies. — [厚労省](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001262636.pdf)
  - Industry body 全終協 planned its first public list of 優良事業者 (approved operators) for 2026-09-25. — [TV Tokyo PR](https://www.tv-tokyo.co.jp/plus/external-pr/entry/245087.html)
- **ペット信託 (pet trust)**
  - Pets are legally "things", so owners use a trust, a burdened bequest (負担付遺贈) or a 死因贈与 instead.
  - Rough initial cost about ¥150,000 for drafting the contract, plus ring-fenced care funds (e.g. about ¥80–90k a year for a cat).
  - Sources: [All About](https://allabout.co.jp/gm/gc/495345/); [三菱UFJ信託 column](https://www.tr.mufg.jp/souzoku-ken/pdf/column_27.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Real, scaled businesses** combine recurring revenue with an everyday need:
  - Frozen meal subscriptions (nosh at about 40M meals a year).
  - Marriage-agency platforms (IBJ, a listed company growing toward ¥31.5B).
  - Solo-tour packaging inside large travel companies.
  - B2G child-support guarantees (municipal subsidy plus insurer backing).
- **Fad or niche:** ソロウェディング, rental family / rental people, solo camping (plateauing), and multi-base subscriptions (ADDress cut prices to ¥980–9,800 tiers).
- **The 終身サポート market:** 400+ unregulated operators plus a new government guideline. This is the "trust gap" pattern: a certified or insurer-backed entrant can stand out.

### Gaps
- Revenue for nosh, 焼肉ライク, オークハウス and マリッシュ (all unlisted or not disclosed).
- Current ワンカラ store count, ひつじ不動産, women-only single housing, JTB solo products, タスカジ/CaSy 2025 figures.
- Japanese adult friend-making apps or circles with user numbers. I did not search ジモティー or 社会人サークル specifically.
- おひとりさま-specific insurance or asset products, and 事実婚/パートナーシップ services.
- イントラスト guarantee contract counts.

## 3. Korea transferability: 1인가구, 비혼/이혼 statistics, existing services

### Takeaway
Korea's single share of households (36.1%, 2024) is already close to Japan's (38%, 2020). Korea's divorce mix is shifting toward 황혼이혼 (divorce after long marriages). That makes Japanese models in remarriage, child-support guarantees, solo meals and lifelong support directly relevant. The Korean data I found on social apps is thin.

### Cited Findings
- **1인가구 (single-person households)**
  - 국가데이터처 (formerly 통계청) 「2025 통계로 보는 1인 가구」, released 2025-12-09: **8.045M single households in 2024, 36.1% of all households**. Both are records; the count is up 2.8% from 7.829M.
  - 70+ is the largest age group, ahead of people in their 20s for the 2nd year.
  - Average income is ₩34.23M a year, 46.1% of the all-household level.
  - 48.9% say they feel lonely, versus 38.2% of all households.
  - Seoul has the highest share at 39.9%.
  - Sources: [뉴시스](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251209_0003433761); [세계일보](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20251209515305)
- By sex and age, the largest single-household groups are men in their 30s and women in their 60s (Sept 2025 report). — [Daum/뉴스](https://v.daum.net/v/20250902122747654?f=p)
- **Marriage and divorce in 2025** (국가데이터처, released 2026-03-19)
  - Marriages about 240k (+8.1%, the 3rd straight year of increase).
  - Divorces about 88k (−3.3%). Another outlet reports 91k (−1.2%), so the figures conflict.
  - The crude divorce rate is 1.7 per 1,000.
  - Divorces after 30+ years of marriage (15,628) exceeded divorces in the first 5 years (14,392) for the first time since records began in 1990. The 30+ year group is the largest share at 17.7%.
  - Divorces among people 65+ rose (men +10.0%, women +9.8%).
  - Sources: [헤럴드경제](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026031822072035707); [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000298493); [노컷뉴스](https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/news/6487592)
- **문토 (Munto, taste-based social meetups app)**
  - 350k+ members, about 90% in their 20s–30s (2022).
  - Model: brokers 소셜링 (one-off meetups), 클럽 (recurring groups) and 챌린지, and sells VOD. Was preparing a 「취향 인연」 matching feature.
  - Revenue is behind a paywall.
  - Sources: [한국경제 2022](https://www.hankyung.com/amp/202209190713i); [The VC](https://thevc.kr/munto); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1535886772)

### Inferences
