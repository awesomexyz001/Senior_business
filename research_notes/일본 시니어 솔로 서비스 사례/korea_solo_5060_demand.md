## Q1. 1인가구: size, age and sex mix, income and assets, isolation, and Seoul/local policy

### Takeaway
Single-person households hit 8.045M (36.1% of households, 2024 basis). 70+ is now the largest age band (19.8%). Among women the 70+ band dominates (29.0% of female 1-person households), and men aged 50–60 are the core isolation and 고독사 risk group: 54% of lonely deaths, and 66.6% of solo 50s lack at least one kind of support network. Income is weak: average 1-person household income is ₩34.23M (46.1% of all households) and assets are ₩223M (39.3%). Public "loneliness" infrastructure is expanding fast and is free, especially in Seoul.

### Cited Findings
**Size and share**
- 2024 basis (published 2025-12-09 by 국가데이터처 in "2025 통계로 보는 1인가구"): 8,045,000 single-person households, 36.1% of all households, the first time above 8M — [korea.kr press release (snippet)](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156734077); [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20251209515043); [Kyunghyang](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202512091341001)
- A different series (인구주택총조사, the census-based count) puts single-person households at 8,244,000, or 36.6% of general households, "as of November last year". The two series use different bases; the snippet did not make clear whether this is Nov 2025 — [newsis / search summary](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251209_0003433761). **Conflict/ambiguity: 804.5만 (2024 basis) vs 824.4만 (later census basis).**
- About 42% of single-person households live in the capital region (수도권) — [베이비뉴스](https://www.ibabynews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=144560)

**Age and sex**
- Age shares (2024): 70+ 19.8% (largest), ≤29 17.8%, 60s 17.6%, 30s 17.4%, 50s 15.1%, 40s 12.3%. 2024 was the first year 70+ overtook ≤29 — [Daum/news summary](https://v.daum.net/v/20251209120019970); [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20251209515043). One outlet says 60+ make up 37.4% (70+ at 19.8% plus 60s at 17.6%) — [dailypop](http://www.dailypop.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=94777)
- By sex: male 1-person households peak in the 30s (21.8%), female ones at 70+ (29.0%) — [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20251209515043)
- Implied 50s+60s share is 32.7% of 8.045M, about 2.6M households (my arithmetic from the shares above). **I found no snippet with a male-only count for 50s–60s.**
- 2023: 2,138,000 older single-person households (65+), 37.8% of older households; 18.7% of them say they have "no one to get help from". Unclaimed deaths (무연고 사망) rose from 3,136 (2020) to 5,415 (2023), up 72.7% — [Segye 2025-02-02 (snippet)](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20250202504125)

**Income and assets**
- Average annual income of 1-person households (2024): ₩34.23M, up 6.2% YoY, which is 46.1% of the all-household average. Outlets report the all-household figure as either ₩72.47M (newsis) or ₩74.27M (Nocut, Kyunghyang), a typo-level conflict — [newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251209_0003433761); [Nocut](https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/news/6440314)
- 53.6% earn under ₩30M a year and 10.6% under ₩10M (from a blog-style summary, so verify) — [gobang.kr](https://gobang.kr/contents/7439)
- Assets: ₩223.02M per 1-person household vs ₩566.78M for all households (39.3%) — [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20251209515305)
- Loneliness: 48.9% of 1-person households say they "feel lonely", more than 10 points above all households; 51.1% are satisfied with their relationships — [newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251209_0003433761)
- Hana Financial Research Institute survey (Apr 2026) of 1,000 working singles aged 25–59 in the capital region and metro cities: 77% want financial products and services designed for 1-person households — [아웃소싱타임스 summarizing 하나금융연구소](https://www.outsourcing.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=203710)

**Middle-aged isolation and 고독사**
- 고독사 (lonely deaths) in 2024 (MOHW survey published 2025-11-27): 3,924 deaths. Men in their 60s are the largest group (1,089, 27.8%), followed by men in their 50s (1,028, 26.2%), together 54%. Men are 80%+ of the total — [newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251127_0003419323); [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000274986)
- 2023: 3,661 고독사 deaths, 53.9% in their 50s–60s and 84.1% male — [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000330748). The National Assembly Research Service found 74.8% of 2017–2023 고독사 victims were aged 40–60s — (search summary citing 국회입법조사처; no direct URL captured)
- MOHW is cutting the 고독사 statistics cycle from 5 years to 1 year. Figures for 2026 are expected in December, and a national "사회적 고립 실태조사" is planned — [newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20251127_0003419323) (snippet)
- National Assembly Futures Institute (국회미래연구원), using the 2025 사회조사 (40–59 household heads; 1,864 single vs 5,091 multi-person households): single households were worse on all 7 isolation indicators. 55.7% of solo 40s and 66.6% of solo 50s lack a support network in at least one domain. Men were worse than women on network absence and group non-participation, and the report flags men in their 50s — [Bravo My Life](https://bravo.etoday.co.kr/view/atc_view/20207) (snippet)
- 한국사회보장정보원: men in their 50s are the largest group of 1-person-household emergency-aid (긴급지원) recipients at 19.0%, and relational disconnection is described as more severe than poverty — [Hankyung 2025-10-13 (snippet)](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025101356781)
- Social isolation rate of about 33% (통계개발원, 2025) — (search summary; URL not isolated)

**Seoul and local policy (free public competitors)**
- 외로움안녕120 is Seoul's loneliness hotline: dial 120, then press 5. It is 24/7, calls are limited to 30 minutes, and there is no cap on the number of calls (Seoul is considering a cap of 5 per day per number). It handled about 13,000 calls in its first ~5 months (2025), hit its annual target within about a month, and reached a cumulative 40,000 calls about one year in (reported June 2026). 61.4% of use is at night, 81% call "to have someone to talk to", and satisfaction is 4.6 out of 5 — [Herald (2026-06-06)](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026060615450071630); [더팩트](https://news.tf.co.kr/read/life/2309640.htm); [Seoul mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2017478)
- 서울잇다플레이스 opened in Seongsu-dong, Seongdong-gu, on Sept 23 as a hub against isolation and reclusion (고립·은둔). It houses 청년마음편의점, face-to-face 120 counselling and an AI mind check; the middle-aged program was listed as starting in October. 서울마음편의점 (local drop-in "mind convenience stores"): 4 opened in 2025, 19 total by 2026 — [Seoul mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2019496); [mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2019227)
- "외로움 없는 서울 시즌2" (Dec 2025) expands programs tailored to the middle-aged — [Seoul welfare news list](https://news.seoul.go.kr/welfare/archives/category/hope-news_c1/loneliness-seoul_c1/loneliness-seoul-news-n1) (headline only). **I could not confirm the commonly cited ₩451.3B five-year budget.**
- Seoul's 사회적고립가구지원센터 (support center for isolated households) runs smart plugs that measure power and light. Since opening in 2022 it monitors 6,000+ households, and Seoul's AI/IoT program covers 45,000 high-risk households — [smartcity.go.kr](https://smartcity.go.kr/?p=39161); [Seoul mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2012885)
- KEPCO's (한전) "1인가구 안부살핌" uses electricity-usage anomalies, with no device needed, since 2021. LG U+ joined with telecom data in 2025. Reported coverage is about 13,000 people in 88 local governments — [Dailian](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1522114/); [etoday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2487108). SKT ran a big-data 안부살핌 service — [inews24](https://www.inews24.com/view/1352749)
- Seoul middle-aged (40–59): about 560,000 are never-married, 20.5% (men 24%, women 17%) — [Nate news 2026-05-07](https://news.nate.com/view/20260507n03588); [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/middle-aged-singles-on-rise-in-seoul-with-money-dividing-happiness/)

### Inferences
- The biggest solo-senior segment by count is older women (70+), but they are lower-income and less digital. The acute isolation problem is men aged 50–60, who are hard to reach and less likely to join groups. A Japanese-style service will need a different channel for each: family-paid for the women, workplace, finance or health channels for the men.
- Single-person households have only about 39% of the assets and 46% of the income of all households, which caps willingness to pay. Services that depend on self-payment by solo 5060s should target the upper income tier, or be paid by family, employer or local government (B2G).
- Seoul and local governments already supply free check-in (KEPCO power data, smart plugs, 120 hotline, AI calls). A private, self-pay check-in product competes directly with free public provision; B2G contracting is the realistic route.

### Gaps
- No snippet gave male counts or income for 1-person households in their 50s and 60s, or women's income at 70+. These need KOSIS tables (블로킹으로 미확인, blocked here so unverified).
- I did not verify the "외로움 없는 서울" total budget (₩451.3B / 2025–2029) or the 2026 YTD call count for 120.
- 2026 고독사 figures are due in Dec 2026 and were not yet available.

## Q2. 비혼/이혼: lifetime never-married rate, gray divorce, remarriage at 50+, attitudes

### Takeaway
Never-married people in their 50s tripled in 10 years to 934,000 (10.9%). The rate is 15.0% for men vs 6.9% for women. Divorces after 20+ years of marriage are about 36–37% of all divorces, and 60+ divorces reached a record 13,743 in 2025. Overall remarriage is falling, but remarriage at 65+ rose about 5–6% in 2025, a growing "second partner" market among older people.

### Cited Findings
- Never-married in their 50s (경제활동인구조사, reported 2026-09-15, likely as of Aug 2026): 934,000, 10.9% of 8.543M people in their 50s, up from 281,000 (3.4%) ten years earlier. Men 15.0%, women 6.9%. Among men the rate is highest in the college-or-less group (19.7%). Among women it rises with education: 5.6% (college or less), 8.9% (4-year degree), 13.5% (graduate) — [Nate news 2026-09-15](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260915n35046); [Nate news](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260915n00460); [karnews](https://www.karnews.or.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=25544)
- A non-official site gives the never-married rate at age 50 (1975 cohort) as 23.6% for men and 11.6% for women. The definition is unclear, so do not mix it with the above — [asing.kr](https://asing.kr/) (low reliability)
- 2025 marriage and divorce statistics (국가데이터처, 2026-03-19): 240,000 marriages, up 8.1%. Divorces by duration: 30+ years 17.7% (largest group), 5–9 years 17.3%, ≤4 years 16.3% — [korea.kr briefing (snippet)](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=156749798); [datanow](https://www.datanow.kr/2026/03/2025-24.html)
- Divorces after 20+ years of marriage: 36.6% of all divorces in 2025 per Hankook Ilbo. A 2024-basis article gives 36.2% — [Hankook Ilbo 2026-10-01](https://www.hankookilbo.com/news/article/A2026100110030004556); [Magazine Hankyung](https://magazine.hankyung.com/business/article/202509027826b)
- 60+ divorces: 13,743 in 2025, up 943, the highest on record. Men 60+ and women in their 50s saw the largest divorce-rate rises — [search summary citing Korean press](https://www.reportera.co.kr/news/twilight-divorce-surge-60s-couples-record-high-2025/); [Korea JoongAng Daily on record gray-divorce share](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/share-of-gray-divorces-hits-record-high-in-q2/12845504). One outlet says the 60+ share of all divorces rose from 13% (2023) to the mid-15% range (2025) (press figure, unverified)
- Remarriage 2025: overall remarriage fell 5.4% for men and 4.4% for women, while remarriage at 65+ rose 5.0% for men and 6.0% for women — [Hankook Ilbo / Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/tKJPoHd8JO)
- Seoul residents' average age at divorce has entered the 50s — [Lady Kyunghyang 2025-09-15](https://lady.khan.co.kr/issue/article/202509151108001)
- Attitudes (2024 사회조사, 2024-11-12): 52.5% say people "should marry", up from 50.0% in 2022 (men 58.3%, women 46.8%). Only 41.6% of never-married men and 26.0% of never-married women agree. 67.4% accept cohabitation (45.9% in 2012). 37.2% accept childbirth without marriage. The top reason for not marrying is lack of money (31.3%) — [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20241125516377); [Dailian](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1428120/); [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000201223)

### Inferences
- The never-married pool in their 50s (about 930,000, about two-thirds of them men) will age into a never-married 60s cohort over 2026–2035. This is structurally similar to Japan's high lifetime-never-married men, about 15–20 years behind.
- The never-married men in their 50s skew low-education, low-income, and the never-married women skew highly educated. Services for single women can price as premium. Services for single men need low price or employer/public funding.
- The combination of record gray divorce and rising remarriage at 65+ supports Japan-style senior remarriage and partner-matching services. Korea's market is still dominated by marriage-agency formats, not low-cost apps (see Q5).

### Gaps
- I found no official Korean projection of the lifetime never-married rate (생애미혼율) at age 50 for 2030–2040. KOSIS 장래가구추계 (household projections) would need to be checked.
- I did not obtain remarriage counts or average remarriage age at 50+ or 60+ for 2025. The report tables exist but the snippets gave no numbers.
- I did not find a recent non-marriage (비혼) attitude survey specifically of people in their 50s and 60s.

## Q3. Retirement-finance demand and Korean competitors

### Takeaway
The 2nd baby-boomer cohort (1964–74, 9.54M) is reaching 60 over 2024–2034. People actually leave their longest-held job at about 52.9 years. Retirement pension (퇴직연금) assets reached about ₩501T (end-2025). Yet 83.5% of accounts that started payouts took a lump sum, which shows a large unmet need for decumulation (drawdown) advice. Robo-advisors for retirement pension accounts only became legal in 2025 and are still small: 핀트 has about 11,700 IRP accounts and about ₩28B of discretionary AUM. Big platforms (토스, 카카오뱅크/페이, 뱅크샐러드) offer aggregation and transfer, not full retirement planning.

### Cited Findings
**Demographics and the retirement wave**
- 2nd baby boomers (born 1964–1974): 9.54M, 18.6% of the population, vs 7.05M (13.7%) 1st boomers (1955–63). They reach the statutory retirement age of 60 over roughly 2024–2034. The Bank of Korea estimates the wave lowers annual GDP growth by 0.38pp over 2024–34 if retirement at 60 stays, and by less than half that with a retirement-age extension — [Hankyung 2024-07-01](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202407013115i); [Bravo My Life](https://bravo.etoday.co.kr/view/atc_view/15508); [NewDaily 2025-01-24](https://biz.newdaily.co.kr/site/data/html/2025/01/24/2025012400287.html)
- BoK says 2nd boomers have more human capital and higher pre-retirement real income and assets than 1st boomers. About 7 in 10 want to keep working after retirement. The average age of leaving the longest-held job is 52.9 (May survey) — [Bravo My Life](https://bravo.etoday.co.kr/view/atc_view/15508); [Nate 2025-07-29](https://news.nate.com/view/20250729n02918)

**Lump sums and the pension "decumulation" gap**
- 퇴직연금 assets: ₩431.7T (end-2024), about ₩501T (end-2025, provisional, up about ₩70T). 2025 return was 6.47%, a record — [Herald Biz](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10939508); [Newsworks](https://www.newsworks.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=903453)
- 2025 payout method by number of accounts: 16.5% annuity, 83.5% lump sum. By amount, annuity is 61.6%. The average lump-sum payout is ₩18.33M. About 82% of annuity choosers set a payout period of 10 years or less. About ₩15T of IRP balances (83.3%) were cashed out before age 55 — [Herald Biz](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10939508); [Newsworks](https://www.newsworks.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=903453)
- 2024: 13.0% of the 573,000 accounts starting payouts chose an annuity (10.4% in 2023) — [Kyunghyang 2025-06-09](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202506092023005)

**Adequacy and preparation surveys**
- 국민노후보장패널 (NPS Research Institute, 10th wave, published Dec 2025): an adequate monthly retirement budget for a healthy couple is ₩2.969M; for an individual the minimum is ₩1.392M and adequate ₩1.976M. People aged 50+ think old age starts at 68.5 — [Hankyung 2025-12-31](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202512315338Y); [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260530504495)
- Average combined 국민연금 (national pension) for couples: ₩1,081,668 a month (Nov 2024), about ₩1.2M per a 2026 MOHW release, against an adequate couple budget of ₩2.98M. In 2025 the first couple receiving over ₩5M a month appeared — [Weekly Kyunghyang](https://weekly.khan.co.kr/article/202504031334001); [insight.co.kr](https://www.insight.co.kr/news/555330) (snippet)
- 보험연구원 (KIRI) survey (3,000 people aged 55–79): 32.5% of retired households ran short of living costs in the past year — [Magazine Hankyung 2026-05-26](https://magazine.hankyung.com/business/article/202605269753b)
- Hana Financial Research Institute "2025 대한민국 금융소비자 보고서": people feel they need about ₩2B of total assets to be secure in retirement — (search summary, no direct URL). **I could not locate the 2025/2026 "대한민국 금퇴 보고서" itself.** The 2026 Wealth Report PDF exists at [pstatic (하나금융연구소)](https://files-scs.pstatic.net/2026/04/15/ypbNvuhaS6/2026%20%EB%8C%80%ED%95%9C%EB%AF%BC%EA%B5%AD%20%EC%9B%B0%EC%8A%A4%20%EB%A6%AC%ED%8F%AC%ED%8A%B8_%ED%95%98%EB%82%98%EA%B8%88%EC%9C%B5%EC%97%B0%EA%B5%AC%EC%86%8C.pdf) (not read)
- KIRI research report 2026-04 on financial capability of older people — [KIRI PDF](http://www.kiri.or.kr/pdf/%EC%97%B0%EA%B5%AC%EC%9E%90%EB%A3%8C/%EC%97%B0%EA%B5%AC%EB%B3%B4%EA%B3%A0%EC%84%9C/nre2026-04_2.pdf) (not read)

**Korean players and traction**
- Retirement-pension robo-advisor discretionary management was allowed from 2025-03-28 under a regulatory sandbox. The first service was Hana Bank with Fount (파운트투자자문). The limit is ₩9M a year per IRP account, rising by ₩9M each year — [Hankyung 2025-03-27](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202503272812i)
- 핀트 (디셈버앤컴퍼니) launched IRP discretionary management in Apr 2025. By Apr 2026 it had 11,674 IRP accounts (discretionary plus advisory) and about ₩28B discretionary AUM. In Jun 2026 it launched with BNK Busan/Kyongnam Bank (minimum ₩1M for ETF, ₩100K for funds). As of Nov 2026 it partners with 8 pension providers (Samsung Securities, Korea Investment, NH, KB Kookmin, IBK, KB Securities, BNK Busan, BNK Kyongnam). These are company-reported figures — [Sports Kyunghyang 2026-04-28](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202604282257003/); [Sports Kyunghyang 2026-06-17](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202606170440013/); [Nate 2026-06-16](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260616n26780)
- 토스: retirement-pension transfer service (Nov 2024). 토스뱅크 with Samsung Securities runs "연금 준비하기", a pension-savings account with robo management. 뱅크샐러드: pension aggregation category including IRP and DC. 카카오페이: national pension and DB retirement-pension lookup announced (2024). 카카오뱅크 is preparing a retirement-pension and AI pension service (Jun 2026, no structure or date fixed yet) — [MTN 2024-11-22](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2024112215281937840); [smarttoday](https://www.smarttoday.co.kr/ko-kr/articles/69166); [MTN 2026-06-08](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026060812405474516)
- Regulation: in 2019 the FSC removed the ₩4B capital requirement for robo-advisor non-face-to-face discretionary contracts, leaving the general discretionary-business (투자일임업) requirement of ₩1.5B. Robo algorithms must pass the Koscom robo-advisor testbed (5,825 products in Aug 2017, 309,506 by Apr 2024). The retirement-pension robo service needed a regulatory-sandbox special exemption — [Ajunews 2019-01-16](https://www.ajunews.com/view/20190116160316812); [Bizwatch 2024-06-05](https://news.bizwatch.co.kr/special/2024/view/202406050035)

### Inferences
- The demand signal is very strong: about ₩500T of pension assets, a 10-year retirement wave of 9.54M people, and an average retired couple's pension covering only about 40% of the "adequate" budget. But the product gap is decumulation planning (how to draw down, when to claim the national pension, tax-efficient annuitization), not accumulation robo. Korea's robo and aggregation players are almost all accumulation-oriented.
- Licensing is a real barrier for Japan-style IFA or retirement-advice businesses: a ₩1.5B capital requirement for discretionary management, the testbed, and sandbox dependence for pension accounts. Non-licensed "information and education" models (calculators, seminars, community) or partnering with licensed 퇴직연금사업자 (pension providers) are the realistic entry paths.
- Retirement finance aimed at single people (no spouse's pension, solo end-of-life costs) is unaddressed. Hana's finding that 77% want products for 1-person households supports a niche.

### Gaps
- I found no survey data on robo-advisor or pension-app usage by people in their 50s specifically.
- I did not find Mirae Asset 투자와연금센터 figures or the Hana "금퇴 보고서" figures (searches returned other reports).
- I did not verify current (2025–26) discretionary-business capital rules or any post-sandbox permanent law change.
- Fount's (파운트) retirement-pension traction numbers were not found.

## Q4. Family-paid parent check-in: demand, paid offerings, free public competition

### Takeaway
Paid check-in products exist but are cheap and small. KT's AI가족안부전화 costs ₩4,950 a month, and the startup 청연케어 안부콜 (Aug 2026) started free. Free public services (KEPCO power-data monitoring in 88 local governments, smart plugs, AI care calls, Seoul 120) set a price anchor near zero. I found no published subscriber numbers for any paid product.

### Cited Findings
- KT/위식스 AI가족안부전화: the guardian sets a time, and the AI calls up to 3 times before alerting the guardian. It covers meal and medication checks and sends weekly or monthly conversation summaries. It costs ₩4,950 a month, billed daily. If there are 2 or more people being protected, they must pay themselves — [familycarecall.com](https://www.familycarecall.com/); [support](https://www.familycarecall.com/support)
- KT 안심박스 (guardian app) costs ₩3,300 a month and is free with kids, junior or senior plans. The protected person must use Android — [KT product page](https://m.product.kt.com/static/prodetail/1426/mobile/itemAccordion/scalable/scalable_1426_01_01_20250109.html); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/kt-%EC%95%88%EC%8B%AC%EB%B0%95%EC%8A%A4/id1549499792)
- SKT NUGU 돌봄 케어콜 (2021) is an AI call service for older people living alone, mostly delivered through local-government contracts. Its current price and status were not confirmed — [Nate 2021-11-03](https://news.nate.com/view/20211103n02438)
- LG U+: no consumer paid check-in price found. It runs B2G telecom-data monitoring of 1-person households with KEPCO — [etoday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2487108); [Dailian](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1522114/)
- 청연 (home-care company) launched "청연케어 안부콜" in Aug 2026. The AI calls daily at a time the child picks, retries up to 2 times, remembers prior conversations, and sends an "안부노트" summary to the child. It was free for applicants until Oct 31, then moves to a monthly fee. The free version allows 5-minute calls, 7am–7pm, no weekends, Android app first, with landline support planned. Its emergency-escalation protocol was not disclosed — [Asiae 2026-08-25](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026082510390849599); [MT/Unicorn Factory](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2026082509320298852); [Venture Square](https://www.venturesquare.net/1108255)
- Free public alternatives: KEPCO 1인가구 안부살핌 (about 13,000 people, 88 local governments, no device needed); Seoul smart plugs (6,000+ households) and AI/IoT coverage (45,000 high-risk households); 외로움안녕120 — see Q1 — [Dailian](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1522114/); [smartcity.go.kr](https://smartcity.go.kr/?p=39161); [Seoul mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2012885)
- Japan comparison (KIHASA): Japanese check-in (見守り) combines IoT with human visits as a middle layer of care — [KIHASA Global Social Security Review](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/gssr/v.12/%EB%B4%84/90/) (title only)

### Inferences
- Korea's check-in market is structured as B2G (local government buys for at-risk low-income seniors) plus cheap telecom add-ons. The Japanese model of an adult child paying ¥1,000–3,000 a month (post-office visits, gas or kettle sensors, Secom) has no scaled Korean equivalent with disclosed traction. Paid products are ₩3–5K a month, about ¥330–550.
- The opening is for middle-class parents who are not eligible for public programs, with the child paying. It would need differentiation (human visits, health escalation, linkage to care services) to justify more than ₩5K a month.

### Gaps
- I found no survey quantifying adult children's worry or willingness to pay for parent check-in in Korea.
- I found no subscriber or revenue numbers for KT AI가족안부전화, 청연, or SKT care call. "T안심" and LG U+ consumer guardian services were not confirmed.
- No smart-plug or electricity-based check-in service for consumers to buy directly (family-paid) was found; the existing ones are public or B2G.

## Q6. Gaps: Japanese-proven service types with no clear Korean equivalent

### Takeaway
The clearest absences in Korea are bundled lifetime-support services for people without family (身元保証・終身サポート: guarantor, daily support and post-death affairs), family-paid check-in with human visits at scale, and retirement drawdown (decumulation) advice for single retirees. Several other formats exist only at early, small scale.

### Cited Findings
- Japan's private 終身サポート services split into three parts: 身元保証 (guarantor for hospital or care-facility admission), 日常生活支援 (daily life support such as accompanying hospital visits) and 死後事務 (post-death affairs such as funeral and estate clearance). The main customers are single seniors without relatives. Japan has had disputes, and only 18.5% of providers kept deposits with trust companies. Japan moved part of this into public social-welfare work in its 2026 revision of the Social Welfare Act; guarantor services and medical decision involvement remain outside it — [Segye 2025-02-02 (snippet)](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20250202504125); [Bravo My Life](https://bravo.etoday.co.kr/view/atc_view/16932)
- Korea: the nearest offering is pre-arranged estate clearance (유품정리 생전예약; 키퍼스코리아, since 2011). Bundled guarantor and post-death delegation services were not found, and Korea is "just beginning to discuss" institutionalization — [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20250202504125) (snippet; article date inconsistent)
- Demand signals in Korea: 18.7% of older people living alone have "no one to get help from" (2023), and unclaimed deaths rose 72.7% from 2020 to 2023 (5,415) — [Segye](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20250202504125)
- Japan's 孤独死保険 (insurance covering cleanup and rent after a lonely death) is spreading — [Asiae EN 2026-05-14](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026051413231998274). Japan's rate of lonely deaths (孤立死) is 62.4 per 100,000 vs 7.7 in Korea, with Korea "following a similar path" — [Daum 2026-04-25](https://v.daum.net/v/20260425180103566)
- Japan's services for pets left without owners have grown fast with solo-senior growth — [Asiae EN 2023-09-04](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2023090410065869734)

### Inferences (gap assessment by Japanese service type)
- **身元保証 / 終身サポート / 死後事務委任 (guarantor and post-death affairs): no clear Korean equivalent.** This is the strongest whitespace, given the 2.14M older people living alone, rising unclaimed deaths, and about 930,000 never-married people in their 50s. Regulation and the safety of trust deposits are key design issues, given Japan's problems.
- **孤独死保険 / tenant lonely-death insurance: not found in Korea.** This is a plausible product for insurers and landlords.
- **Family-paid check-in with human visits (Japan Post or Secom style):** only ₩3–5K AI-call add-ons and B2G programs exist in Korea. A partial gap.
- **Retirement decumulation advice for solo retirees:** only aggregation and accumulation robo exist, constrained by licensing. A gap.
- **50+ dating and friendship apps:** 시놀/시럽 exist at about 110K members. A partial gap, in scale and trust.
- **Senior-solo travel and events:** 시놀 travel membership and 아너드 exist, both early. A partial gap.
- **5060 fashion commerce:** 퀸잇 (4050 women) and 하프클럽 exist. Covered for women; services for men 5060 are not evident.
- These assessments rest on search coverage, not exhaustive market scans. Absence in the search results does not prove absence in the market.

### Gaps
- I did not search Korean private guarantor (신원보증) providers, hospital-admission guarantor rules, or pending bills in depth, so the "no equivalent" finding needs one more targeted check.
- I did not check Korean lonely-death insurance or pet-succession products directly.
