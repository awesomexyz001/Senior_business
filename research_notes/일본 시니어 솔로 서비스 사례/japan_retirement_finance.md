# Japan: Retirement Finance and Senior Money Management Services (退職金・老後資金・資産寿命・認知症と資産管理), as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-19. Method: web search plus snippets. Many news and startup-database sites could not be fetched (kepple.co.jp failed DNS in this session's network allowlist), so a large share of the figures are **snippet-only (SO)**: they come from search-engine summaries of the linked page, not from reading the page. Items marked [SO] should be checked before going into a final report. Items marked [TK] come from my training knowledge (cutoff mid-2026) and were not re-verified in this session.

## Q1. Policy drivers that created demand

### Takeaway
Japan's demand comes from a chain of policy events: the 2019 "老後2000万円" report made retirement drawdown a public issue. 新NISA (2024) pulled tens of millions into investing, and people in their 50s hold the most accounts. Expanded iDeCo (from Dec 2026, age cap raised to under 70) lengthens the accumulation window. Meanwhile ~128兆円 of financial assets held by people with dementia (2025) creates a "frozen asset" problem. The 成年後見制度 overhaul (2026) and the FSA's 顧客本位 and 金融ジェロントロジー guidance push financial institutions to buy solutions. The "200兆円 by 2030" figure is a 2018 estimate. Dai-ichi's July 2026 update is lower: 127.8兆円 in 2025 and 196.6兆円 around 2040.

### Cited Findings
**老後2000万円問題 (June 2019)**
- The 金融審議会 市場ワーキング・グループ report 「高齢社会における資産形成・管理」 (2019) estimated that a retired, unemployed elderly couple runs a deficit of about ¥50,000 per month on average. That implies drawdowns of about ¥13M over 20 years and about ¥20M over 30 years. Newspaper coverage and social media turned it into the "2000万円問題". [SO] — [NLI research PDF](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/topics/75900_ext_18_0.pdf?site=nli); [Toyo Keizai](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/289562?display=b); [ITmedia blog](https://blogs.itmedia.co.jp/business20/2019/06/2000.html)
- Commentators note the figure is an average, not a universal shortfall, and argue that the controversy did not durably improve household money-management skills. [SO] — [gendai.media](https://gendai.media/articles/-/149512); [moneypost](https://www.moneypost.jp/784789)

**新NISA (from Jan 2024): uptake by age**
- Per FSA 「NISA口座の利用状況調査（2025年12月末時点）」: 28.21M accounts, up 10.2% from 25.59M at end-2024. **People in their 50s hold the most accounts, 5.53M**, the largest age group in both 2024 and 2025. [SO] — [money-bu-jpx (JPX)](https://money-bu-jpx.com/news/article071768/4/)
- People in their 60s: about 3.77M (2024) to about 4.18M (2025), roughly +11%. This was **read from a chart in the article, not from a stated number**, so treat it as unverified. [SO] — [money-bu-jpx](https://money-bu-jpx.com/news/article071768/4/)
- Accounts grew by 4.33M in 2024 and slowed to 2.61M in 2025 (NLI). [SO] — [NLI PDF](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/topics/82741_ext_18_0.pdf?site=nli); also [DLRI flash](https://www.dlri.co.jp/report/asset/629200.html)

**Household assets of people in their 60s (J-FLEC 「家計の金融行動に関する世論調査」2025; J-FLEC took over from 金融広報中央委員会/知るぽると in 2024)**
- Two-or-more-person households in their 60s, including households with no financial assets: mean ¥26.83M, **median ¥14.00M**. [SO, secondary] — [LIMO](https://limo.media/articles/-/132119)
- Counting only households that hold assets: 60s couples have a mean of ¥30.87M and a median of ¥17.75M. 60s single-person households have a mean of ¥19.78M and a median of ¥9.13M. [SO, secondary] — [All About](https://allabout.co.jp/gm/gc/520848/)

**iDeCo reform (enacted 2025, effective 1 Dec 2026)**
- The age cap rises from under 65 to **under 70**. It mainly covers people who are already iDeCo members or 運用指図者, or who are moving assets in from corporate pensions, and who are not yet drawing 老齢基礎年金 or iDeCo benefits. A transitional rule lets other people aged 60–69 join until the end of Nov 2029. [SO] — [Monex 2026-05-14](https://info.monex.co.jp/news/2026/20260514_01.html); [Matsui FAQ](https://support.matsui.co.jp/faq/show/56257)
- Contribution caps: employees without a corporate pension go from ¥23,000 to **¥62,000 per month**. For employees with a corporate pension, the separate ¥20,000 cap is removed and a combined ¥62,000 cap applies. Self-employed go from ¥68,000 to ¥75,000. The changes apply from contributions debited in Jan 2027. Sources disagree on the exact application date (2026-12-01 effective vs 2027-01-26 debit). [SO] — [Daiwa LP](https://www.daiwa.jp/lp_dc/ideco/202612ideco/); [SOICO](https://www.soico.jp/no1/news/securities/5728); [Resona](https://www.resonabank.co.jp/nenkin/ideco/amendment.html)

**金融ジェロントロジー and 顧客本位**
- The FSA raised 金融ジェロントロジー in its July 2018 「高齢社会における金融サービスの在り方（中間的な取りまとめ）」. The Aug 2020 市場WG report asked institutions to expand safe, convenient service for elderly customers with reduced cognitive capacity and their families, from a 顧客本位 standpoint, drawing on 金融ジェロントロジー. [SO] — [NLI PDF](https://www.nli-research.co.jp/files/topics/65668_ext_18_0.pdf?site=nli); [DIR 2018](https://www.dir.co.jp/report/research/capital-mkt/it/20181107_020425.pdf)
- For depositors with reduced cognitive capacity, banks are expected to follow 全銀協's 「金融取引の代理等に関する考え方」. This guidance (2021) allows limited withdrawals by relatives for the customer's own benefit, such as medical and care costs, without a guardian. That ruling is the basis for banks' 代理人 services. [SO] — [MHLW PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12000000/001107538.pdf); the 2021 date is [TK]
- From Nov 2024, financial service providers have a statutory duty to act in the customer's "最善の利益". [SO, from an IFA advertorial] — [adviser-navi](https://adviser-navi.co.jp/asset-management/column/57985/)

**Dementia and frozen assets (認知症と資産凍結)**
- Dai-ichi Life Research (2018, 星野卓也): financial assets held by people with dementia were about ¥140兆 in March 2018 and were projected to exceed **¥200兆 by 2030**. Another version: ¥127兆 (2015) to ¥215兆 in FY2030, 10.4% of household financial assets. [SO] — [DLRI 2018 PDF](https://www.dlri.co.jp/pdf/dlri/04-20/1807_1.pdf); [DLRI macro 157663](https://www.dlri.co.jp/report/macro/157663.html); [President](https://president.jp/articles/-/26858)
- **Updated estimate (DLRI, 2 July 2026, 福澤涼子・河谷善夫・星野卓也)**: ¥127.8兆 in 2025, 6.0% of household financial assets, rising to **¥196.6兆 (7.6%) around 2040**. The report argues that keeping dementia patients' assets in deposits erodes their real value when prices are rising. The PDF credits the estimate to "第一ライフ資産運用経済研究所", and I could not confirm whether that name is a renaming or a different entity. [SO] — [DLRI report 623706](https://www.dlri.co.jp/report/asset/623706.html); [PDF](https://www.dlri.co.jp/files/asset/623706.pdf)
- Daiwa Institute of Research estimated about ¥126.6兆 at end-2023, on a different basis. [SO] — [DIR 2024-12-20](https://www.dir.co.jp/report/research/capital-mkt/asset/20241220_024811.html)
- MOF's Policy Research Institute notes that from 2030 onward, more than 10% of Japanese household assets could be "frozen". [SO] — [MOF PRI seminar 2025-06-27](https://www.mof.go.jp/pri/research/seminar/fy2025/lm20250627.pdf)

**成年後見制度 reform (2026)**
- On 27 Jan 2026 the 法制審議会 subcommittee approved a draft outline (要綱案). It abolishes lifetime guardianship. Support can be limited to specific acts and ended partway. The three categories 後見, 保佐 and 補助 are merged into one category based on 補助. The family court grants the 補助人 powers over specified acts, subject to three conditions: insufficient capacity, the person's consent, and need. [SO] — [秋田魁新報 2026-01-27](https://www.sakigake.jp/news/article/20260127CO0087/)
- The 法制審 plenary (第204回, 12 Feb 2026) dealt with the 要綱. [SO] — [TKC 2026-02](https://www.tkc.jp/consolidate/tkc_express/2026/02/202602_13430). One explainer says the Cabinet approved the bill in **April 2026**, but this is **not confirmed by a primary source**. — [HT tax guide](https://www.ht-tax.or.jp/sozoku-guide/adult-guardianship-reform-2026)

### Inferences
- The reforms move a little demand away from court guardianship toward lighter, more flexible tools. But they also make guardianship less of a "last resort to avoid". That could reduce the urgency that 家族信託 marketing ("後見になると一生やめられない") has relied on. Watch how 家族信託 companies change their messaging after the law takes effect.
- 新NISA plus iDeCo to 70 puts large invested balances in the hands of people in their 50s and 60s. That is where "decumulation" (取り崩し) tools come in, and brokers began adding them in 2025–26 (see Q2).
- The lower DLRI 2026 estimate (¥196.6兆 around 2040) should replace the over-cited "200兆円 by 2030" in any pitch, or both should be cited with their dates.

### Gaps
- The exact 60s NISA account number and the 70+ figure from the FSA primary table could not be fetched.
- The Diet passage date and effective date of the 成年後見 reform bill are unconfirmed as of Nov 2026.
- I did not fetch the original text of the 全銀協 2021 guidance.

## Q2. Concrete Japanese cases (company / service / launch / user vs payer / pricing / scale / what worked)

### Takeaway
The clearest scaled businesses are owned by incumbents: trust banks' dementia-ready trusts, brokers' free 定期売却 features, MUFG's ¥997億 purchase of WealthNavi, and listed And Do HD (リースバック) and Kamakura Shinsho (相続/終活 portals). Pure-play startups (ファミトラ, おやとこ, AGE technologies) raised meaningful VC money (おやとこ ¥18.1億 in Jan 2024; ファミトラ about ¥7億 in Jan 2024). They grow mainly through **B2B2C partnerships** with insurers, card companies and financial institutions, combined with high-ticket one-off consulting fees plus a small monthly SaaS fee. No startup in this space has disclosed profitability.

### Cited Findings

**A. 家族信託 (family trust) platforms**
- **ファミトラ (株式会社ファミトラ)**: founded Nov 2019 by 三橋克仁, a serial founder whose earlier company manabo was sold to Sundai in 2018. The 2021 Series A investors included Aflac Ventures and 東京海上日動. [SO] — [THE BRIDGE 2021-12](https://thebridge.jp/2021/12/famitra-series-a-round-funding). It raised about **¥7億** in Jan 2024. [SO] — [起業手帖 2024-01-17](https://sogyotecho.jp/news/20240117famitra/)
  - Pricing: setup consulting from ¥100,000 (¥110,000 incl. tax), varying with the size and type of assets and the time to sign. The ongoing 運用サポート costs ¥1,480/month (¥1,628 incl. tax), billed yearly. Separate costs: lawyer fees for the contract, notarization, 信託口口座 opening, registration tax and 司法書士 fees. Its own column puts the market rate for design at 1–1.5% of trust assets and registration at about ¥100,000 per jurisdiction. [SO] — [SBI証券 expert page](https://go.sbisec.co.jp/consult/expert/expert_detail_7825.html); [SBI column 2025-04-30](https://go.sbisec.co.jp/consult/inheritance_column/column_detail_9614.html)
  - B2B2C channel partners: JFR Card (大丸松坂屋, Aug 2021); エムアイカード (三越伊勢丹 card, Dec 2022, member perks offered to 2.17M cardholders); 大樹生命 (Aug 2025), which bundles ファミトラ as a 付帯サービス with a dementia-related insurance rider; capital-alliance talks with 三越伊勢丹イノベーションズ (date unclear). [SO] — [ASCII](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/264/4264130/); [paymentnavi](https://paymentnavi.com/?p=125628); [voix](https://voix.jp/money/insurance/11299/)
  - The company presents itself on SBI証券's 相続 consultation portal, which is another referral channel. [SO] — [SBI expert page](https://go.sbisec.co.jp/consult/expert/expert_detail_7825.html)
- **おやとこ (株式会社こころのカンパニー, formerly トリニティ・テクノロジー; also ran 「スマート家族信託」)**: combines 家族信託 consulting with a SaaS for managing trust assets. It raised **¥18.1億** on 17 Jan 2024, aiming for about 3,000 おやとこ contracts in FY2024. It advertises "家族信託契約件数3年連続No.1", based on a 日本マーケティングリサーチ機構 survey, which is a marketing-style ranking and not an audited count. From 2 July 2026 it launched 「ちゃりとら」 to support setting up public-interest trusts (公益信託). [SO] — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000119.000077937.html); [起業手帖 2024-01-17](https://sogyotecho.jp/news/20240117trinity-tech); [THE BRIDGE Series A1 2022-06](https://thebridge.jp/2022/06/trinity-technology-series-a1-round-funding)
  - No IPO found. Pricing not found in search. [SO]
- 家族信託 is a private contract, so the total number of contracts cannot be measured. [SO] — [toushi.homes](https://toushi.homes.co.jp/column/?p=11911)

**B. Bank and trust-bank dementia-ready products (代理人 / 見守り)**
- **三菱UFJ信託銀行 「つかえて安心」** (launched 2019): a money trust with an app, where family members approve or monitor payments for medical, care and living costs. At launch, minimum ¥2M; setup fee 1.5% (excl. tax) on the portion up to ¥50M; ¥480/month. A 2021 schedule shows 1.65% (incl. tax) up to ¥50M and 1.1% above, minimum ¥110,000 and maximum ¥1.65M, plus ¥528/month. Withdrawals and transfers are free and the app is free. [SO] — [MUTB release 2019-01-17](https://www.tr.mufg.jp/ippan/release/pdf_mutb/190117_1.pdf); [MUTB release 2021-03-18](https://www.tr.mufg.jp/ippan/release/pdf_mutb/210318_1.pdf); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id1452748792)
- **「みらいのまもり」 is a 三菱UFJ信託銀行 product, not 三井住友信託.** The brief's attribution looks wrong. It is a trust account with withdrawal restrictions, payable only for nursing-home entry fees or medical bills of ¥100,000 or more, requested by the person or a 受益者代理人, with no cash withdrawal. [SO] — [MUTB mamori](https://www.tr.mufg.jp/mamori/)
- **三井住友信託銀行 「人生100年応援信託〈100年パスポートプラス〉」**: a 手続代理人 chosen in advance, such as a family member, can cancel and re-subscribe fund-wrap or mutual-fund holdings after the client's capacity declines. [SO] — [SMTB](https://www.smtb.jp/personal/entrustment/passport_plus/service)
- **みずほ信託 (2019 product)**: requires ¥5M or more in trust. After a dementia diagnosis, if no 手続代理人 is available, the customer can name up to three presumptive heirs as contacts. If none are reachable, the bank alerts the 地域包括支援センター to consider guardianship. [SO] — [Mizuho release 2019-09-03](https://www.mizuhobank.co.jp/release/pdf/20190903release_jp.pdf)
- Megabanks' "代理人予約サービス" cover deposits only and are described as a simple dementia measure. No 2025 Mizuho launch was confirmed. [SO, personal blog] — [libecity](https://library.libecity.com/articles/01JY5XG7PKDSJQBNZYB8AQ2KBE)
- Brokers: SMBC日興 has a dementia-related program under its 高齢化社会 CSR page. [SO] — [SMBC Nikko](https://www.smbcnikko.co.jp/csr/business/koreikashakai/ninchisho/04.html)

From **6 Dec 2025** it added **定率指定 (0.1–50% in 0.1% steps)** and **期間指定 (up to 50 years, choosing the final payout month)**, plus 定期売却 inside NISA accounts. - **楽天証券**: offered 定期売却 from end-2019, usable inside new NISA (both つみたて and 成長 allowances). Payouts are monthly, on a chosen day between the 1st and 28th. [SO] — [money-lifehack](https://money-lifehack.com/article/22102); [Gentosha GOLD](https://gentosha-go.com/articles/-/77362?page=3)

More than 500,000 users (end-Mar 2026), AUM about ¥2兆 (May 2026) and about ¥2.1兆 (July 2026). [SO] — [HRMOS company page](https://hrmos.co/pages/wealthnavi); [paiza](https://paiza.jp/recruiters/3560)

  - Implication: the standalone robo-advisor was bought by a megabank and folded into a B2B2C distribution model.

**F. リバースモーゲージ / リースバック**
- **ハウスドゥ (And Doホールディングス, 3457)**: FY6/2025 consolidated revenue ¥64,735M (-4.2%), operating income ¥2,620M (-27.0%), net income ¥2,341M (-5.5%). **G. 相続 (inheritance) digital services and 終活×金融**
- **AGE technologies**: its 「そうぞくドットコム不動産」 handles real-estate title transfer online. It launched Jan 2020, with more than 22,000 cumulative registrations. - **NTTデータ関西 「TSUGI+」**: a cloud service that lets families file one inheritance application across multiple financial institutions online. It was planned for nationwide rollout to financial institutions from **April 2026**. This is a B2B model where the institution pays. [SO] — [電波新聞 2026-01-16](https://dempa-digital.com/?p=705360)
- **鎌倉新書 (6184)**: runs 「いい相続」 (inheritance portal, launched June 2020), 遺産相続弁護士ガイド, an estate-clearing referral service and others. The 相続 segment was not broken out. The model is lead generation: professionals and businesses pay referral fees and the consumer is not charged. ### Inferences
- **Payer pattern**: (1) Trust banks charge the senior 1–1.65% upfront plus about ¥500/month, which is profitable as part of a large AUM base. (2) 家族信託 startups charge the adult child or family ¥100,000+ upfront plus about ¥1,500/month, and grow through insurers and card companies as B2B2C. (3) Brokers give 取り崩し features away to keep AUM. (4) Portals such as Kamakura Shinsho earn referral fees from professionals. (5) Institution-paid SaaS: NTT Data's TSUGI+, and おやとこ's SaaS layer.
- **What worked**: being attached to an existing pool of trusted assets (banks, MUFG–WealthNavi), lead generation that monetizes life events (Kamakura Shinsho), and distribution through insurance riders (大樹生命 × ファミトラ). **What looks hard**: standalone B2C startups facing a high-ticket, one-off, legally complex sale. 家族信託 needs lawyers, notaries and 司法書士, which limits how far software alone can scale, and no startup has disclosed profitability. Leaseback carries reputation and regulatory risk (Kokusen complaints, MLIT rules from Oct 2026).
- The adult child (40s–50s) is usually the one who starts the purchase for dementia and inheritance products, while the senior's assets fund them. For a Korean 5060 target, which includes both parents of 80+ and their own retirement, the "dual role" user is the sweet spot.

### Gaps
- I found no confirmed contract counts, revenue or profitability for ファミトラ, おやとこ or AGE technologies, and no 2025–26 funding for them.
- I found no Money Forward data (users aged 50+, 「マネーフォワード 相続」 type services), no 50+ user share for THEO / お金のデザイン, and no ねんきんネット usage statistics in this session.
- The specific services named in the brief, 「相続ナビ」, 「ツナグ相続」, 「相続手続きオンライン」 and 楽天 「終活」, were **not found** under those names. I could not confirm that they exist or what scale they have.
- I found no contract counts for つかえて安心, みらいのまもり or 100年パスポートプラス.

## Q3. Korea transferability (brief) and what blocks a small startup

### Takeaway
Korea's comparable incumbents are the state-run 주택연금 (HF), banks' 유언대용신탁 (4.5조원 at the top 5 banks, end-2025) and the 퇴직연금 system. Regulated activities such as trust, discretionary management and investment advice require licences or registration with capital floors, so a small software team should copy the **non-licensed layers** of the Japanese cases. Those layers are: family-coordination and monitoring SaaS on top of bank trusts (おやとこ's SaaS / つかえて安心's app model), decumulation calculators and simulators that do not advise on specific products, and inheritance-procedure workflow and lead generation (Kamakura Shinsho / TSUGI+), sold B2B2C to banks and insurers.

### Cited Findings
- **주택연금 (HF)**: 136,146 cumulative subscribers (2024), under 2% of eligible households. New subscribers held around 14,000 per year: 14,580 (2022), 14,885 (2023), 14,670 (2024), 13,925 (2025). [SO; exact source among the results not attributed] — [경향신문 2025-09](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202509012040005/); [Economist KR 2026-07](https://economist.co.kr/article/view/ecn202607100001)
- **유언대용신탁** at the top 5 commercial banks grew from 9,405억원 (end-2020) to **4조5,023억원 (end-2025)**. [SO] — [한국경제 2026-07-16](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2026071601331) / [세계일보 2026-09](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260904511889) (attribution between these unclear)
- **하나은행 「내집연금」** (private reverse mortgage for homes with an official price above 12억원): launched May 2025. Commitments exceeded 3,300억원 from about 260 subscribers. [SO] — [헤럴드경제 2025-08-07](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2025080715244597556)
- **Licensing**: 투자자문업 requires registration with equity of at least 1억원, varying by business unit: 2.5억원 for securities and derivatives advice, 1억원 for fund-only advice, plus qualified staff (sources differ). 신탁업 and 투자매매·중개·집합투자업 require **authorization (인가)**. A robo-advisor must meet the 전자적 투자조언장치 requirements, including passing the testbed and quarterly rebalancing. A 2019 proposal to drop the 40억원 equity requirement for non-face-to-face discretionary contracts, leaving 15억원, has an unconfirmed final status. [SO, mostly blogs and 2016–2019 docs] — [FSC](https://www.fsc.go.kr/no010101/72210); [아주경제 2019-01-16](https://www.ajunews.com/view/20190116160316812); [help-me.kr](https://www.help-me.kr/guide/detail/%EA%B8%88%EC%9C%B5%ED%88%AC%EC%9E%90%EC%9E%90%EB%AC%B8%EC%97%85)

### Inferences
- **Blockers for a small startup**: (1) It cannot hold or manage money in trust without 신탁업 인가, so it cannot copy 家族信託 or the trust-bank products directly. Korea also has no well-established non-bank "civil 家族信託" practice like Japan's, which needs legal confirmation. (2) Any personalized product recommendation, such as "move your 퇴직금 into fund X", triggers 투자자문업 registration with a capital floor plus staff requirements. (3) Reading users' accounts across banks needs a 마이데이터 licence or partnership (본인신용정보관리업 허가). Licensing details were not verified in this session. (4) 주택연금 is a state monopoly with a weak take-up of about 2%, which leaves room for an **education and simulation front-end** but not for a competing product.
- **Most transferable Japanese patterns**: (a) the Japanese decumulation features (SBI 定率/期間, Dec 2025) as a free, non-advisory planner app that hands off to brokers' or banks' 연금 accounts (IRP, 연금저축) for affiliate or lead fees; (b) a family-coordination and monitoring app for parents' dementia preparedness, sold to banks with 유언대용신탁 and 치매안심신탁 products, which grew 4.8× in five years; (c) a digital inheritance-procedure workflow and professional-matching portal modelled on 鎌倉新書 いい相続 and TSUGI+, sold to banks and insurers.
- Leaseback (ハウスドゥ) is a poor fit for a software team: it needs a real-estate balance sheet and carries consumer-protection risk, as shown by Japan's rising complaints and MLIT's Oct 2026 rules.

### Gaps
- Current 신탁업 인가 capital requirements, 마이데이터 licence conditions, and whether 2019's robo-advisor relaxations were enacted are all unverified.
- I found no data on Korean 치매신탁 balances specifically, or on 퇴직연금 디폴트옵션 uptake among people in their 50s and 60s.
- I could not determine whether Korean civil law and practice support a Japan-style 家族信託 (non-bank individual trustee) at scale. Legal review is needed.
