## 1. Demographics: single-elderly households, never-married rates and late-life divorce (熟年離婚)

### Takeaway
社人研's April 2024 household projection has single-person households reaching about 44.3% of all households by 2050, with 65+ single-person households at about 10.84M. The 50-year never-married rate in 2020 was 28.3% for men and 17.8% for women (imputed values). Divorces after 20+ years of living together were about 40.7k in 2024. All three trends grow the pool of older people with no family to fall back on.

### Cited Findings
- 社人研 published its 2024 national household projection (令和6年推計) in April 2024. It covers 2020–2050 and uses the 世帯主率法 method (snippet) — [IPSS HPRJ2024](https://www.ipss.go.jp/pp-ajsetai/j/HPRJ2024/t-page.asp); full report: [hprj2024_houkoku.pdf](https://www.ipss.go.jp/pp-ajsetai/j/HPRJ2024/houkoku/hprj2024_houkoku.pdf)
- Single-person households are projected at **44.3% of all households in 2050**. This 社人研 figure is cited in a 内閣府 「安心・つながりPT」 reference document (snippet) — [内閣府 PT 参考資料](https://www.cao.go.jp/kodoku_koritsu/torikumi/project_team/dai5/pdf/sankou.pdf)
- **65+ single-person households are projected at 10.84M in 2050**, 1.5M more than the previous projection (snippet) — [日本総研 JRI](https://www.jri.co.jp/page.jsp?id=107780)
- By prefecture in 2050:
  - In 21 prefectures, households headed by someone 65+ will be over 50% of all households.
  - In 32 prefectures (道府県), 65+ single-person households will be over 20% of households.
  - The 65+ living-alone rate (独居率) will be over 20% everywhere except Yamagata, and over 30% in 5 prefectures (都府県).
  - The number of 75+ single households in 2050 will be at least double the 2020 number in Okinawa, Shiga, Saitama and Ibaraki.
  - (snippet) — [PSRN](https://www.psrn.jp/topics/detail.php?id=33465); [kaiketsu-j](https://www.kaiketsu-j.com/?p=13093)
- IPSS 人口統計資料集 table 7-12 shows single-person households at 21.15M in 2020 and about 23.3M in 2050. The snippet does not say which projection vintage this comes from (snippet) — [IPSS 表7-12 (2026)](https://www.ipss.go.jp/syoushika/tohkei/Data/Popular2026/T07-12.files/sheet001.htm)
- Current level: in 2024, single-person households were **31.8% (about 8.79M) of households that include someone 65+**. This is quoted from the 高齢社会白書 by a secondary article; the white-paper edition was not verified (snippet) — [gentosha-go](https://gentosha-go.com/articles/-/81877)
- **50歳時未婚率 (formerly called 生涯未婚率), 2020:**
  - Men 28.25%, women 17.81% (IPSS 人口統計資料集).
  - In 2015 it was men 24.77%, women 14.89%.
  - These are 不詳補完値: people with unknown marital status are allocated proportionally. Under the older method that excludes unknowns, 2020 comes out at men 25.7%, women 16.4%.
  - (snippet) — [Newsweek Japan (舞田敏彦)](https://www.newsweekjapan.jp/articles/-/45948); [生命保険文化センター](https://www.jili.or.jp/lifeplan/lifeevent/805.html)
- Over the longer run, the 50-year never-married rate rose from about 9% to 28% for men and from about 5% to 18% for women between 2000 and 2020. JRI notes that older people with no relatives to rely on will keep increasing (snippet) — [JRI](https://www.jri.co.jp/page.jsp?id=107780)
- **熟年離婚 (divorce after living together 20+ years):**
  - 2024: 40,686 couples, with 20–24 years the most common band.
  - Share of all divorces: 21.5% in 2020 (another article says 21.6%), the highest since 1950; 23.5% in 2022.
  - In court statistics, cases between couples married 20+ years were 26% of the 2024 family-court total. This is a different measure.
  - (snippet) — [gentosha-go 72186](https://gentosha-go.com/articles/-/72186); [allabout](https://allabout.co.jp/gm/gc/517083/); [IPSS 表6-13](https://www.ipss.go.jp/syoushika/tohkei/Data/Popular2025/T06-13.files/sheet001.htm)
- **孤立死 (dying alone at home):**
  - 内閣府 announced on 2026-04-14 that it estimates **22,222 such deaths in 2025** (+366 on 2024), of whom **15,911 were 65+**.
  - Definition: single people who died at home and were found 8 or more days after death. Based on 警察庁 data; suicides are included.
  - (snippet, 共同通信) — [熊日](https://kumanichi.com/articles/1980476); [さきがけ](https://www.sakigake.jp/news/article/20260414CO0121/)

### Inferences
- The core target is the 65+ single household (about 10.84M by 2050). Within it, never-married men are the fastest-growing group with no family at all: in 2020 the men's never-married rate was about 1.6x the women's, so this cohort will age into the 65+ group in the 2030s–2040s.
- Late-life divorce adds a group that has children but is estranged from them. This matches Korean data showing that about 75% of 무연고 deaths had relatives who refused to claim the body (see section 6).

### Gaps
- I could not open the 2024 projection report itself. The national 2050 count of 65+ single households should be checked against [hprj2024_houkoku.pdf](https://www.ipss.go.jp/pp-ajsetai/j/HPRJ2024/houkoku/hprj2024_houkoku.pdf). The 2020 base figure (about 6.7M) is from memory and is **not cited**.
- I found no projected count of never-married people aged 65+.
- I did not confirm whether 2024 was a record year for the late-life-divorce share.
- I found no 2026 高齢社会白書 figures.

## 2. Policy: the 2024 sector guideline, the 2024 isolation law, the 2025 rental-housing amendment, consumer complaints, and 2026 updates

### Takeaway
The legal framework moved in three steps:
1. **June 2024:** a soft-law guideline for private lifelong-support companies (終身サポート事業者). There is still no licence or registration requirement.
2. **April 2024 and October 2025:** the 孤独・孤立 law took effect (April 2024), and the amended 住宅セーフティネット法 created certified 居住サポート住宅 rental housing (October 2025).
3. **June 2026:** an amended 社会福祉法 made support for people with no family a 第2種社会福祉事業 (a category of social-welfare service), delivered mainly by 社会福祉協議会 (社協, local social-welfare councils) at free or low cost for low-income people.

Alongside this, the industry association 全終協 started a voluntary certification and certified its first 8 companies on 2026-09-25.

### Cited Findings
**高齢者等終身サポート事業者ガイドライン (June 2024)**
- Jointly issued by 内閣官房 (身元保証等高齢者サポート調整チーム), 内閣府孤独・孤立対策推進室, 金融庁, 消費者庁, 総務省, 法務省 and 厚労省 — [Guideline PDF (法務省 mirror)](https://www.moj.go.jp/content/001420018.pdf); 厚労省 notice to local governments, 介護保険最新情報 Vol.1273 (2024-06-11) — [mhlw](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001262630.pdf)
- The guideline says the government will consider creating a scheme to certify good-quality providers (優良事業者認定) (snippet) — [moj.go.jp guideline](https://www.moj.go.jp/content/001420018.pdf)
- As of October 2026, an industry explainer says there is still no national qualification, permit or registration needed to start a 身元保証 business (snippet) — [akarihosho column 970](https://www.akarihosho.jp/column/970/)
- Local governments are actively promoting the guideline. For example, さいたま市's page was updated 2026-03-05 (snippet) — [さいたま市](https://www.city.saitama.lg.jp/002/003/003/002/005/p115565.html)

**Industry self-certification (2026)**
- 一般社団法人全国高齢者等終身サポート事業者協会 (全終協) runs the scheme. Its chair is 黒澤史津乃 of OAGウェルビーR. A document submitted to 厚労省 describes it:
  - It adds 107 review items on top of the national checklist.
  - External reviewers hold face-to-face interviews, and the board decides pass or fail.
  - Only certified companies become full members (正会員).
  - **First round: 8 companies certified, 2026-09-25.**
  - (snippet) — [mhlw 001756938.pdf](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12602000/001756938.pdf); [zenshukyo guideline page](https://www.zenshukyo.org/guideline/)

**Amended 社会福祉法 (passed June 2026)**
- Passed on 2026-06-19 (secondary sources).
- It expands the existing 日常生活自立支援事業 (the daily-living support programme for people with dementia and similar) into a 第2種社会福祉事業 run mainly by 社協.
- Under a contract with the person, it provides help with hospital or care-home admission procedures, everyday money management, and after-death affairs (死後事務).
- It is free or low-cost for low-income older people.
- (snippet) — [akarihosho column 1911](https://www.akarihosho.jp/column/1911/); [akarihosho column 1566](https://www.akarihosho.jp/column/1566/); [シルバー新報 (interim plan)](https://silver-news.com/blog/detail/2706); [商工法務G](https://sh-g.jp/info/news/1748/)
- **Caveat:** the passage date and the start date (施行日) come only from secondary law-firm and company sites. The 厚労省 primary PDF could not be reached.

**孤独・孤立対策推進法**
- Took effect April 2024. A 孤独・孤立対策推進本部 (headquarters) was set up in 内閣府 (snippet) — [健康長寿ネット](https://www.tyojyu.or.jp/net/topics/tokushu/koritsushitakoreisha-seikatsuosasaeru/koreishagakoritsushinaishakainojitsugen.html); [内閣府 PT 報告書](https://www.cao.go.jp/kodoku_koritsu/torikumi/project_team/pdf/houkokusho.pdf)

**Amended 住宅セーフティネット法 (amended June 2024, in force October 2025)**
- It creates **居住サポート住宅** (legal name: 居住安定援助賃貸住宅). 居住支援法人 (designated housing-support bodies) work with landlords to provide safety checks, monitoring and links to welfare services. The 市区町村長 (heads of municipalities that run a welfare office) certify these homes.
- It adds handling of a deceased tenant's belongings (残置物処理), on the tenant's instruction, to the tasks 居住支援法人 may do. It also expects use of the 2021 MLIT model contract clauses for 残置物 handling.
- It simplifies approval of 終身建物賃貸借 (lifetime building leases) and creates a 認定保証業者 (certified rent-guarantor) scheme.
- (snippet) — [MLIT](https://www.mlit.go.jp/jutakukentiku/house/content/001966220.pdf); [板橋区](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/tetsuduki/sumai/seido/1063302.html); [世田谷区](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/03665/27455.html)
- Uptake has been slow: **124 certified 居住サポート住宅 units as of 2026-01-13** (snippet) — [高齢者住宅財団 居住支援サミット資料 (2026-01)](https://www.koujuuzai.or.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/r7KyojuShienSummit2.pdf)

### Inferences
- From 2026 there are two tiers: a public 社協 tier for low-income people, and a private paid tier for the middle class. Private providers now compete on certification (全終協) and deposit safety rather than on simply being available. This is a useful model for Korea, where nothing like either tier exists yet.
- In housing, policy is moving risk away from landlords through the 居住支援法人 handling belongings, certified guarantors, and monitoring. But only 124 units were certified in 15 months, so the scheme has not yet unlocked supply at scale.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the start date (施行日) of the amended 社会福祉法, or whether it requires private providers to register or notify (届出). The primary 厚労省 document was blocked.
- I found no PIO-NET complaint counts after FY2021.
- I found no official 2026 revision of the guideline. It appears unchanged since June 2024.
- I found no 居住サポート住宅 count after January 2026.

## 3. Cases: Japanese companies and organisations (身元保証, 死後事務, 終活, housing, digital)

### Takeaway
The sector is fragmented: about 400 providers by one count, mostly NPOs, 一般社団法人, 行政書士 and 司法書士 offices, and funeral-related companies. Large brands such as Aeon, 日本郵便 and 鎌倉新書 sit at the referral and funeral end rather than holding deposits themselves. 日本ライフ協会's 2016 collapse is the defining trust failure. It made third-party management of deposits (預託金) the main design question.

### Cited Findings
**日本ライフ協会 (collapse, 2016)**
- A 公益財団法人 in Minato-ku with about **2,500 members**.
- It collected about ¥880M (8.8億円) in deposits, of which about ¥270M (2.7億円) was missing.
- It won public-interest status in 2010 by advertising a three-party contract in which a lawyer held the deposits. It then switched to a two-party contract under its own control and lent the money to related entities.
- It applied for civil rehabilitation (民事再生) at the Osaka District Court. The planned transferee, (一社)えにしの会, withdrew, and the case became bankruptcy.
- Three former officers were arrested under the 出資法 (Investment Act) for taking about ¥20M (約2,000万円) in "deposits" from about 40 people between April 2014 and June 2015 without permission.
- (snippet) — [gendai.media](https://gendai.media/articles/-/51263); [data-max](https://data-max.co.jp/article/9814); [消費者委員会 2019-08-30 報告](https://www.cao.go.jp/consumer/iinkaikouhyou/2019/doc/20190830_houkoku1.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Three business models are visible:**
  - **Deposit-holding membership** (Aeon, きずなの会, the failed 日本ライフ協会): ¥1–1.7M up front.
  - **Professional contract packages** (トゥモローズ): about ¥1M, with after-death fees settled from the estate and no deposit.
  - **Referral platforms** (鎌倉新書 with 日本郵便): free to the user, monetised through partner commissions.
- The listed company 鎌倉新書 is profitable mainly on funeral, grave and altar referral, not on guarantees. This suggests the lead-generation layer is where software margins are, while guarantees carry liability.
- Housing is the most acute everyday pain point: about half of management companies refuse older tenants. Monitoring plus insurance bundles (R65) sell to landlords, not to the older person.

### Gaps
- I found no data on LifeEnding Technologies, よりそう (よりそうお葬式), JA 終活 services or 安心サポート (prices, users, funding). The searches returned nothing usable.
- I found no member or user counts for きずなの会, Aeon 身元保証, or R65's registered landlords and properties.
- I found no data on insurance products aimed at people living alone (おひとりさま向け保険), such as small-sum funeral or 孤独死 insurance for tenants.
- I found nothing on women-focused shared houses or コレクティブハウス (for example コレクティブハウスかんかん森).
- 鎌倉新書's segment split (funerals vs. 相続 vs. care) and its 2026 investor-relations figures on 終活 or おひとりさま were not available.

## 4. Business economics: prices, market size, trust problems and who pays

### Takeaway
A typical lifelong-support contract costs **¥1M–¥1.7M**, much of it as an up-front deposit. The market for 身元保証 plus 生前整理 (clearing belongings while alive) is about **¥23.5B (FY2024 estimate) → ¥25.7B (FY2025 forecast) → about ¥28B (FY2026 forecast), growing about 9% a year** (矢野経済研究所, May 2025). The older person pays, which pushes the market toward people with savings. From 2026, low-income people are meant to be served by 社協 for free or at low cost.

### Cited Findings
- 矢野経済研究所 (press release May 2025):
  - Market for 身元保証 + 生前整理, measured by end-user spending: **¥23.45B in FY2024 (estimate)** and **¥25.73B in FY2025 (forecast, 109.7% of the previous year)**.
  - Commentators cite a **FY2026 forecast of about ¥28B**, with about 9% average annual growth.
  - (snippet) — [矢野経済 press 3807](https://www.yano.co.jp/press-release/show/press_id/3807); [womanslabo](https://womanslabo.com/news-women-250526-3); [note (行政書士)](https://note.com/yuji_trast/n/nff1921dc6ca3)
- Industry overview, prices and number of providers:
  - Commonly quoted all-in cost for a 身元保証 company: ¥1M–¥1.5M (snippet) — [caresul-kaigo](https://caresul-kaigo.jp/column/articles/18783/)
  - Aeon: from ¥1,416,296 or from ¥1,696,400 depending on the page (see section 3).
  - トゥモローズ: from ¥1,045,000.
  - About 400 providers, according to the search summary. The 総務省 2023 survey is the likely origin, but the attribution is unverified (snippet) — [総務省 2023-08](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000895038.pdf)
- Deposits are needed because the provider cannot collect payment from the person after death, so funeral and interment costs are prepaid. How the deposit is managed is the key check (snippet) — [caresul-kaigo](https://caresul-kaigo.jp/column/articles/18783/)
- Trust failure: 日本ライフ協会 had about ¥270M (2.7億円) missing out of about ¥880M (8.8億円) in deposits (snippet) — [gendai](https://gendai.media/articles/-/51263)
- Who pays:
  - Private services: the older person pays.
  - New 社協 service: free or low-cost for low-income people (snippet) — [akarihosho 1911](https://www.akarihosho.jp/column/1911/)
  - 孤独死 insurance: usually the landlord pays the premium (snippet) — [健美家](https://www.kenbiya.com/ar/ns/for_rent/hoken/3788.html)
  - Local governments subsidise landlords' insurance (snippet) — [新宿区](https://www.city.shinjuku.lg.jp/seikatsu/jutaku01_000001_00003.html)

### Inferences
- At roughly ¥1.2M average per contract, a ¥25B market implies on the order of 20k new contracts a year. This is a rough back-of-envelope figure, not a sourced number. It is small next to the 65+ single population, which suggests most older people with no family are not served by private providers.
- Products that do not hold deposits (estate-settled fees, trust banks, escrow) are the main lesson from 日本ライフ協会. In Korea, 상조 prepaid-fund regulation under 할부거래법 (protection of prepaid instalments) could act as an analogue.

### Gaps
- I could not open the 矢野 primary release. The FY2026 forecast of about ¥28B comes from secondary commentary, and the release date of 2025-05 is inferred from the press_id and article dates.
- I found no average contract value, churn or cancellation-refund statistics.

## 5. Which pieces are software, and which need human or legal service

### Takeaway
The digital pieces in the Japanese market are planning and end-of-life notebooks, ID and password and message vaults, monitoring based on utility data, and referral platforms. The core service, acting as guarantor for hospital or care-home admission and carrying out 死後事務, is still contract-based and delivered by people (lawyers, 行政書士, NPO staff, 社協).

### Cited Findings
- Digital vault plus messages to family (lastmessage); My Number-linked end-of-life notebook aiming to automate procedures after death (SouSou) — [創業手帳 puzzle ring](https://sogyotecho.jp/news/20240515puzzle-ring/); [創業手帳 SouSou](https://sogyotecho.jp/?p=217557)
- The number of subscriptions and contracts that must be cancelled after death keeps growing (monthly services, phone lines), so people need a way to tell family about them (snippet) — [創業手帳](https://sogyotecho.jp/?p=217557)
- Monitoring without a device: alerts from electricity usage data, bundled with insurance (R65) — [横浜市](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/business/bunyabetsu/kenchiku/torikumi/safetynet/safety-mimamori.files/0105_20260603.pdf)
- Referral model: the 日本郵便 counter refers to 鎌倉新書's provider network (more than 1.5M cumulative referrals) — [鎌倉新書](https://www.kamakura-net.co.jp/newstopics/8405/)
- Human or legal core: the new 社協 service covers admission procedures, money management and 死後事務 under a personal contract (snippet) — [シルバー新報](https://silver-news.com/blog/detail/2706). Packages bundle legal instruments such as 死後事務委任 and 身元保証 contracts (トゥモローズ) — [tomorrowstax](https://tomorrowstax.com/gh/blog/tomorrows-ohitorisama-package/)
- Certification needs auditable compliance: 全終協 checks 107 items on top of the national checklist (snippet) — [mhlw 001756938](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12602000/001756938.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Can be software:**
  - Intake and needs assessment, plus a guided end-of-life notebook.
  - A vault for documents and accounts.
  - Contract-lifecycle and compliance dashboards for providers (checklists aligned with the guideline and 全終協).
  - Escrow and deposit-tracking transparency.
  - Notification on death: detection through monitoring or a hospital/社協 trigger, then a cancellation workflow.
  - Matching to professionals (lawyers, 行政書士, funeral, belongings clean-up).
  - Monitoring APIs for landlords.
- **Needs people or a legal entity:**
  - Signing as guarantor at admission.
  - Emergency attendance.
  - Medical-decision support.
  - Funeral and interment.
  - Clearing the home and belongings.
  - Probate and estate settlement.
- For a small Korean team, a B2B SaaS layer for providers (contract, deposit and compliance management) or a B2B2C referral platform carries less liability than becoming a guarantor.

### Gaps
- I found no Japanese SaaS built specifically for 終身サポート providers, and no data on how widely the digital tools are adopted (download or user counts).

## 6. Korea transferability (brief)

### Takeaway
Korea has the same underlying problem and a weaker institutional base. 무연고 deaths (deaths with no one claiming the body) roughly doubled from 2021 to 2025 to about 7k, but there is no national statistic. 공영장례 (public funerals) is left to local government discretion. Seoul's 1인가구 (single-person household) policy covers health, moving and emotional support, but I found no lifetime-guarantee or after-death-affairs service, public or private.

### Cited Findings
- 무연고 사망 (unclaimed deaths):
  - 6,139 in the previous year (2024 implied), per 보건복지부 data reported by 뉴시스 on 2025-09-05 — [newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20250905_0003318050)
  - **2021: 3,603 → 2025: 7,171**, per 권칠승 의원실 data — [supple.kr](https://supple.kr/news/cmu31ibux003z33d1a7ymth3q)
  - The two sources' 2024 figures do not match.
- 2025: **5,370 cases (74.9%)** had relatives who refused or avoided taking the body. 65+ 무연고 deaths rose from 1,845 (2021) to 4,310 (2025), +133.6% — [supple.kr](https://supple.kr/news/cmu31ibux003z33d1a7ymth3q)
- 2024: 1,318 (21.5%) died in a home rather than a medical facility — [newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20250905_0003318050)
- 공영장례 is at local discretion, so it varies by region.
  - Seoul's public funerals grew from 864 to 1,496 (period not stated in the snippet).
  - Incheon grew from 99 (2021) to 488 (2025).
  - There are no national statistics because handling is left to 기초 지자체 (basic local governments).
  - (snippet) — [supple.kr](https://supple.kr/news/cmu31ibux003z33d1a7ymth3q); [newsis](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20250905_0003318050); [대구시](https://www.daegu.go.kr/welf/index.do?menu_id=00941120)
- Social cost when funeral responsibility goes unclaimed (2026-09-27) — [머니투데이](https://www.mt.co.kr/article/2026092719121473519)
- Seoul:
  - 1인가구 동행서비스 combines health (hospital escort), moving and emotional support (마음 동행). Apply through the 1인가구 portal or the 일인친구 call centre (1533-1179) — [서울 미디어허브](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2017809)
  - A 5-year 1인가구 master plan with ₩600B (6천억원) committed for 2026, including more agencies dedicated to isolated households — [헤럴드경제 2026-03-16](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026031618160234178); [서울시 복지](https://news.seoul.go.kr/welfare/archives/577727)
- Korean private market:
  - 1인가구 are 36.1% of households; 고독사 (dying alone) 3,661 in 2023.
  - Belongings clean-up (유품정리) is growing. Advance booking for it was introduced by 키퍼스코리아 in 2011, and 한국유품정리사협회 had about 30 member firms (year unclear).
  - 메모리올 is an end-of-life preparation platform (August 2025).
  - (snippet) — [maily 커버링](https://maily.so/peoplewhy/posts/g0zml603rql); [시대 2025-08-29](https://www.sidae.com/article/2025082914211115970)

### Inferences
- Korea's 74.9% relative-refusal rate means the market includes people who have family but are estranged, not only those with no family. A Korean 사후사무 위임 (after-death affairs mandate) product would therefore need a legal route to override or skip claims by next of kin. This would need legal review under the Korean Civil Act's mandate (위임) and inheritance rules.
- Points of entry in Korea that mirror Japan:
  - 지자체 공영장례 and 1인가구 centres, as the counterpart to 社協.
  - 상조 companies, as the counterpart to Aeon and the funeral-led model.
  - Landlord and 임대 risk products, as the counterpart to R65.
- Korea has no guideline-equivalent yet. A first mover that builds in Japan-style deposit escrow and a public compliance checklist could gain trust.

### Gaps
- I found no Korean private lifetime-guarantee (신원보증) service for hospital or care-home admission, and no statutory 사후사무 위임 product.
- I found no review of Korean local ordinances on 1인가구 or 공영장례, such as which 조례 include after-death support, and no national 1인가구 policy measures.
- I did not get 2025 Korean 고독사 statistics.
