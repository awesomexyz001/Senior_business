## 1. ハルメク (Halmek): magazine + commerce + community + events for women 50+ (key case)

### Takeaway
Halmek is the best-documented profitable 50+ women's platform in Japan. It was rebuilt after a 2009 civil rehabilitation into a subscription-only magazine (no newsstand sales; about 460k copies, Japan's No.1 selling magazine) that acts as a customer-acquisition and trust engine for catalog/online commerce, department-store shops, events, courses and a paid digital membership (ハルメク365). Group revenue is about ¥34bn and operating profit is rising fast (¥1.07bn in FY3/2025 → ¥1.77bn in FY3/2026). The active customer base, however, is shrinking (1.37M → 1.27M), mainly in the acquired ことせ catalog business.

### Cited Findings
**History / turnaround**
- The predecessor company filed for civil rehabilitation (民事再生) in 2009. 宮澤孝夫 was brought in to rebuild it and became president. At its peak the earlier title 『いきいき』 had about 430k copies. It was renamed 『ハルメク』 in 2016 despite internal opposition. [S] — [gendai.media 長谷川あや「民事再生からV字復活を遂げた究極のシニアビジネス」](https://gendai.media/articles/-/63981); [日経 宮沢孝夫氏 先入観排し企業を再建 (2023)](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUC094KH0Z00C23A6000000/)
- The "V字回復" framing refers to FY2017 revenue of ¥10.3bn. About 230k paid copies were reported as of April 2019. [S] — [gendai.media](https://gendai.media/articles/-/63981)
- The company sends commerce catalogs to both current and past subscribers, and catalog circulation is about 2x the subscriber count. Commerce is a core profit pillar. [S] — [日本流通産業新聞 宮澤社長40周年インタビュー](https://online.bci.co.jp/article/detail/121)

**Circulation / subscription mechanics**
- Sold only by subscription, never in bookstores or convenience stores. Single issues cannot be bought. [S] — [paymentnavi「年額一括払い」でも部数を拡大](https://paymentnavi.com/paymentnews/143783.html); [dokode365](https://dokode365.com/halmek/)
- Price: ¥7,800 for 12 issues (1 year) and ¥18,900 for 36 issues (3 years), shipping and tax included. [S] — [ハルメク公式 定期購読コース](https://magazine.halmek.co.jp/guide/course/)
- Subscribers passed 500k with the Dec 2022 issue. [S] — [PR TIMES (Halmek)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000098.000034765.html)
- Circulation of 467k was cited in a FY3/2025 results article (2025). [S] — [日本流通産業新聞 online 2910](https://online.bci.co.jp/article/detail/2910)
- H2 2025 (Jul–Dec): 459k copies, No.1 in sales among all Japanese magazines for 8 consecutive half-year periods (since H1 2022), per a June 2026 release. [S] — [PR TIMES (Halmek) 8半期連続No.1](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000269.000034765.html)
- Caveat: sources mix 実売部数 (paid sales), 発行部数 (print run) and 定期購読者数 (subscribers), so the figures are not strictly comparable.

**Financials (ハルメクホールディングス, TSE Growth 7119)**
- FY3/2025: consolidated revenue ¥33.93bn (+8.0%), operating profit ¥1.068bn (+24.5%), net profit ¥623M (+31%). The ハルメク segment had revenue of ¥26.75bn (+11.3%), growing new customers through newspaper ads and stores. [S] — [日本流通産業新聞 online 2910](https://online.bci.co.jp/article/detail/2910)
- FY3/2026: revenue ¥33.812bn (−0.3%), operating profit ¥1.774bn (+66.1%), net profit ¥1.051bn. The revenue decline came from ことせ物販 (fewer catalog recipients, −¥960M). The ハルメク business grew ¥410M on magazine price increases and higher merchandise sales. **Customers fell from 1.37M to 1.27M (−7.5%).** [S] — [ネットショップ担当者フォーラム 2026/05/19](https://netshop.impress.co.jp/n/2026/05/19/16085); [みんかぶ 7119 決算](https://minkabu.jp/stock/7119/settlement)
- H1 FY3/2026 (to Sep 2025): revenue ¥16.78bn vs ¥16.92bn the prior year. H1 operating profit +32.0%, attributed to structural reform of ハルメク merchandise. [S] — [ログミーFinance 386114](https://finance.logmi.jp/articles/386114)
- Guidance for FY3/2027: revenue ¥35.0bn (+3.5%), operating profit ¥2.0bn (+12.7%). [S] — [netshop.impress 16085](https://netshop.impress.co.jp/n/2026/05/19/16085)
- Mid-term plan (May 2025): revenue ¥35.0bn (FY3/27), ¥37.5bn (FY3/28), ¥40.0bn (FY3/29). Operating profit ¥1.5bn → ¥2.0bn → ¥2.5bn (2.5x vs FY3/25), with ROE of 15% by FY3/28. The growth axes are three: **情報コンテンツ (content) × 物販 (commerce) × コミュニティ (events/courses)**. In the May 2026 briefing the targets were restated as "¥40bn revenue / ¥2.5bn OP by FY3/28", so the framing changed between years. [S] — [ログミーFinance 381649](https://finance.logmi.jp/articles/381649); [日本流通産業新聞 3年後売上400億円](https://online.bci.co.jp/article/detail/5700); [みんかぶ/ログミー 2026/05/20](https://minkabu.jp/news/4522785)
- Implied operating margin: about 3.1% in FY3/2025 and about 5.2% in FY3/2026 (calculated from the figures above).

**Digital and community**
- ハルメク365 is a paid membership launched in 2022 (articles, videos, online courses, events tied to the magazine). The magazine e-edition began streaming on 365 in March 2023. [S] — [Halmek HD press 365 1周年](https://www.halmek-holdings.co.jp/news/press/2023/l04qlv_v3/); [PR TIMES 電子版配信](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000101.000034765.html)
- Fees: in March 2023 it was ¥6,600/yr for subscribers and ¥9,240/yr for non-subscribers. From 2023-10-01 it changed to **¥1,980/yr for magazine subscribers and ¥7,800/yr for non-subscribers**, which effectively turned it into an add-on for the existing base. Online courses are included ("参加し放題" for a limited period). [S] — [paymentnavi](https://paymentnavi.com/paymentnews/143783.html)
- No ハルメク365 member count was found in any snippet.
- ハルメク 生きかた上手研究所 is an in-house think tank that surveys readers and feeds product development. [S] — [Halmek HD press 2024 あべのハルカス](https://www.halmek-holdings.co.jp/news/press/2024/s2keearacc-c/)

**Physical stores (ハルメク おみせ)**
- The 11th directly run store opened at あべのハルカス近鉄本店 in March 2024. In July 2024 there were 14 stores including pop-ups. A 2025 release says "全国17店舗". 伊勢丹立川 (August 2025) was the 6th store in Tokyo. The stores sit mainly in department stores and won the 2024年度 百貨店バイヤーズ賞 レディス部門 新人賞 for sales staff service ("神接客"). [S] — [Halmek HD press 2024](https://www.halmek-holdings.co.jp/news/press/2024/s2keearacc-c/); [Halmek HD press 2025](https://www.halmek-holdings.co.jp/news/press/2025/1y558tbcik6b/); [PR TIMES 立川](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000207.000034765.html); [ECzine Day 2024 session](https://event.shoeisha.jp/eczday/20240827/session/5172)

### Inferences
- What makes it work: (1) a paid subscription magazine as the trust and data layer, paid for upfront (annual prepay), which costs little to acquire through newspaper ads; (2) commerce of products co-developed from reader research (fit, comfort, small-pain-point items) sold to a known, addressable list; (3) the community layer (events, courses, 365) mainly raises retention and lifetime value and is not a standalone profit center; (4) offline department-store shops recruit new customers who don't buy by mail.
- The economics are commerce-led, with low single-digit operating margins. A Korean team should not expect "community" alone to monetize. Halmek's community is a loss-leader or retention tool wrapped around commerce and subscriptions.
- The digital membership was repriced to ¥1,980 for subscribers. This suggests standalone digital demand at ¥7,800–9,240 was weak, and that bundling with the paper product is what drives uptake. This is inferred from the price change, not stated by the company.
- Customer-count erosion (−7.5%) while profit rose suggests pruning of low-value catalog recipients (ことせ). Acquisition is the structural challenge even for the category leader.

### Gaps
- ハルメク365 member count and ハルメクカルチャー (courses/events) revenue and participant numbers were not found. The IR PDFs on jpx.co.jp were blocked.
- Segment profit split (magazine vs commerce vs stores vs events) and digital ad revenue / ハルメクWEB PV were not found.
- Exact current store count (late 2026) was not found.

## 2. Senior SNS and community platforms: 趣味人倶楽部, らくらくコミュニティ

### Takeaway
Japan's two largest senior SNSs reached only about 0.4M (趣味人倶楽部) and about 3.4M (らくらくコミュニティ) members after 15+ years. Both make money mainly from advertising and sponsored research and tie-ups, not from user fees. らくらくコミュニティ's scale comes from distribution bundled on らくらくスマートフォン handsets.

### Cited Findings
- **趣味人倶楽部 (しゅみーとくらぶ):** launched in 2007, co-created with クラブツーリズム and originally run by DeNA. It is now run by オースタンス. [S] — [日経クロストレンド「平均57.5歳 元気なシニアのSNS」](https://xtrend.nikkei.com/atcl/contents/18/00257/00004/); [月刊ネット販売](https://nethanbai.co.jp/archives/4535)
- Membership passed 400k (PR TIMES, 2025-02-13). Recruiting copy (Wantedly, 2025) says "会員42万人・月間3,500万PV". [S] — [Excite/PR TIMES 2025-02-13](https://www.excite.co.jp/news/article/Prtimes_2025-02-13-18508-143/); [Wantedly オースタンス](https://sg.wantedly.com/companies/ostance/about)
- Ages 50–70 make up 75% of members. It is anonymous and organized around hobbies. About 100k people a year join real-world and online events (offline meetups organized by members). An average member age of 57.5 was reported by Nikkei Xtrend (date not confirmed). [S] — [マーケメディア 広告メニュー](https://note.com/marke_media/n/n05625ff9258a); [日経クロストレンド](https://xtrend.nikkei.com/atcl/contents/18/00257/00004/)
- The model is ad menus, research and surveys (for example, senior YouTube usage surveys and 敬老の日 spending webinars). [S] — [commercepick シニアYouTube調査](https://www.commercepick.com/?p=61060); [Web担当者Forum/PR TIMES ウェビナー](https://webtan.impress.co.jp/r/prtimes/items/000000169.000018508)
- **らくらくコミュニティ (FCNT):** 3.1M members (January 2025) and 3.4M (end of March 2026) per media guides. It targets ages 60–80s. It was relaunched as a "fan community service" in October 2019. The i-mode feature-phone version ended in November 2017, while the smartphone and PC versions continue. [S] — [bizpa 媒体](https://bizpa.net/media/171/); [FCNT media guide 2025](https://www.fcnt.com/business/snsad/pdf/rakucomu_mediaguide_25.pdf); [FCNT 2019 news](https://www.fcnt.com/news/detail/20191030.html); [FCNT i-mode終了](https://www.fcnt.com/consumernotice/1016/)
- It is ad-funded (media guide for advertisers). The © on the 2024–2025 media guides reads "FCNT LLC". [S] — [FCNT media guide 2024](https://www.fcnt.com/business/snsad/pdf/rakucomu_mediaguide_24.pdf)

### Inferences
- Even the "largest" senior SNSs are small next to the 50+ population (tens of millions). An ad-only model gives modest revenue, and the sustainable value is as a research and sampling panel for brands.
- らくらくコミュニティ's 3.4M is a distribution effect (on-device on senior handsets), not organic SNS virality. A Korean equivalent needs a distribution partner (carrier, device, bank, or an apps like 캐시워크 that seniors already use).
- 趣味人倶楽部's main engagement asset is offline meetups (about 100k/yr). This suggests online is the matching layer and offline is where value is felt.
- (Background, unverified in this session:) FCNT went through civil rehabilitation in 2023, and its assets went to a Lenovo-backed "FCNT LLC". The "FCNT LLC" copyright on the guides is consistent with this, but it should be verified.

### Gaps
- Revenue and profitability of オースタンス and of らくらくコミュニティ were not found.
- MAU vs registered members for both were not found. Registered counts likely overstate active use.

## 3. Travel and membership clubs: 大人の休日倶楽部 (JR東日本), クラブツーリズム

### Takeaway
Big incumbents with a core transaction (rail tickets, package tours) built very large 50+ membership bases: about 3M for 大人の休日倶楽部 and about 2.5M households receiving クラブツーリズム's monthly 「旅の友」 magazine. They then layered on hobby and learning programs and peer-group formation (テーマ旅行, ひとり参加 tours). The membership works because it carries a hard economic benefit (discounts) or a concrete activity (travel), not "community" in the abstract.

### Cited Findings
- **大人の休日倶楽部:** 1M members (2009-02-13). ジパング alone was 1.248M (2016-04-01). It reached 2.5M (Dec 2019). Cumulative members are about 3M (December 2025, per the jeki 2026 media kit). Another jeki ad page says about 2.71M (date unstated). [S] — [JR東日本 press 2009](https://www.jreast.co.jp/press/2008/20090210.pdf); [JR東日本 press 2016](https://www.jreast.co.jp/press/2016/20160404.pdf); [JR東日本 press 2019](https://www.jreast.co.jp/press/2019/20191216_ho02.pdf); [jeki 媒体資料 2026](https://www.jeki.co.jp/files/otona.pdf); [jeki LP](https://online-soudan.jeki.co.jp/senior-otona_jreads-ad-lp1/)
- Tiers: Middle (ages 50–64) costs ¥2,100 + ¥524 card fee = ¥2,624/yr (first year free), with 5% off. Zipangu (65+) costs ¥3,840 + ¥524 = ¥4,364/yr, with 30% off JR East/Hokkaido tickets of 101km+ one-way (other sites say 201km). New 夫婦会員 sign-ups ended on 2024-04-01. [S] — [JR東日本 年会費・入会条件](https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/otona/guide/); [tabi-yasu 2026](https://tabi-yasu.com/otona-kyujitsu-club/)
- It also sells the members' magazine plus Suica-data-targeted ads through jeki, so the member base is monetized as a media asset too. [S] — [jeki LP](https://online-soudan.jeki.co.jp/senior-otona_jreads-ad-lp1/)
- It runs "趣味の会" (hobby classes) through JR東日本びゅうツーリズム&セールス. [S] — [jre-vts 大人の休日倶楽部 趣味](https://www.jre-vts.com/business/otona_hobby/)
- **クラブツーリズム:** the monthly 「旅の友」 reaches about 2.5M member households (recruiting page, undated). Its business includes テーマのある旅 aimed at 仲間づくり (peer formation) and lifelong learning, plus ゆったり旅 (frailty-friendly). [S] — [マイナビ2027 会社概要](https://job.mynavi.jp/27/pc/search/corp62147/outline.html)
- In FY2011, 66% of tour participants were in their 60s or older. テーマ旅行 includes accompanying experts and lecturers. [S] — [President](https://president.jp/articles/-/9074)
- ひとり旅 tours (everyone travels solo): about 1,500 courses a year and about 35,000 participants (2014). Separately, a survey found most senior solo travelers "debut" in their 50s (2013). [S] — [トラベルボイス 2014](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20140209-16501); [トラベルボイス 2013](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20130503-4705)
- KNT-CT HD FY3/2025: revenue ¥274.5bn (+7.5%), operating profit ¥6.04bn (−16.9%). クラブツーリズム alone was about ¥124bn (trade-press figure, about 130% YoY), with テーマ旅行 at 115% YoY. A パーソナルデザイン旅行センター (unescorted themed independent travel) opened in April 2025. [S] — [travelvision](https://www.travelvision.jp/news/detail/news-116566); [KNT-CT 決算短信 2025](https://www.kntcthd.co.jp/files/ja/ir/library/results/r06_03tanshin_0513.pdf); [観光経済新聞 2025](https://www.kankokeizai.com/2506030800kntct/)
- KNT-CT plans to integrate with 近畿日本ツーリスト and others in April 2027 (per the recruiting page). [S] — [マイナビ](https://job.mynavi.jp/28/pc/search/corp62147/outline.html)

### Inferences
- In Japan, "solo seniors who want companions" are most profitably served through activity products (themed solo tours) that build community as a side effect, rather than through SNS. A Korean product could lead with paid group experiences (trips, classes) and use the app for discovery and repeat bookings.
- A low annual fee plus a tangible benefit (discount) produces very large member bases. Those bases then become an ad and media asset.

### Gaps
- Current クラブツーリズム member count, ひとり参加 share, and テーマ旅行 revenue size were not found.
- 大人の休日倶楽部 趣味の会 participant numbers were not found.

## 4. Learning: カルチャーセンター, online lessons (ストアカ), スマホ教室, men's places, 大人のスナック

### Takeaway
Traditional culture centers are a shrinking, senior-skewed market (about ¥33–36bn). Online skill marketplaces skew 30–50s and female, with seniors appearing more as teachers than learners. Carrier smartphone classes have huge reach (Docomo: 20M+ cumulative), but they are free customer-retention tools. Spaces for retired men remain small, local and volunteer-run, and no scaled business model was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Culture-center market (矢野経済研究所):** FY2020 ¥22.6bn (60.6% of the prior year), FY2021 ¥27.2bn, FY2022 ¥33.0bn (expected, +21.3%), FY2023 ¥36.0bn (forecast). It faces competition from specialist schools and university and public courses, and online is seen as the new channel. [S] — [矢野経済研究所 2023](https://www.yano.co.jp/press/press.php/003242); [dreamnews](https://www.dreamnews.jp/press/0000279157/)
- NHK文化センター: FY3/2022 students −1.9%, which is −44% vs FY3/2020. Revenue was ¥3.259bn (−46.1%). 朝日カルチャーセンター revenue was ¥1.956bn (−37.5%). Nikkei: "受講生に占めるシニアの割合は高まっているが、売り上げは伸び悩み" (the senior share of students is rising but sales are stagnant). Students per facility peaked in 2002. [S] — [日経業界解説](https://www.nikkei.com/telecom/industry_s/0893); [大阪府資料](https://www.pref.osaka.lg.jp/documents/30624/culture.pdf)
- **ストアカ:** more than 5,000 pre-senior and senior teachers. A survey of 293 users aged 50+ (October 2025) found about 30% of people in their 50s want a different field after retirement, about 30% use learned skills for side income, AI skills are the most popular theme, and about half of teachers in their 60s report 生きがい. Users are about 80% female, centered on ages 30–50s (2026 infographic). [S] — [ストリートアカデミー release 2025-11-11](https://corp.street-academy.com/news/release20251111); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000083.000007608.html); [ストアカ note 2026](https://note.com/street_academy/n/n4079654d37b8)
- MHLW: self-development participation falls with age, and people 50+ more often cite "hard to find course information". [S] — [MHLW PDF](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11801000/001478801.pdf)
- **ドコモスマホ教室:** more than 20M cumulative participants by end of July 2025. It is expanding to generative AI and kids' courses. In 2018–2020 there were 9M+ participants, about 90% aged 60+. In 2023–24 it ran 800+ outreach sessions in 90+ municipalities without shops. [S] — [NTTドコモ 2025-09-04](https://www.docomo.ne.jp/binary/pdf/info/news_release/topics_250904_x0.pdf); [総務省 2021](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000735753.pdf)
- **Men's places:** a 男の料理教室 at a たつの市 community center has run 33 years, with 17 participants aged 60s–80s in FY2025. 松戸's 「男のためのサロン 暮らし助け合いタイ」 had 8 participants (July 2025). Since FY2009 鳴門市 has run municipal cooking classes for men 65+ for care prevention. Most are municipal or volunteer efforts with no fee data. [S] — [神戸新聞 2025-05](https://www.kobe-np.co.jp/news/seiban/202505/0019024907.shtml); [ちいコミ 松戸](https://chiicomi.com/press/2081037/); [徳島新聞/Yahoo](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/6a3c215d6dabe695a0848b9d177b187c078c83b0)
- **昼スナック / salons:** 赤坂 「スナックひきだし» opens daytime with rotating hosts (ママ/マスター), and customers come to talk rather than drink or sing. It started in 2017 by borrowing an existing snack bar's space, growing from 2x/month to weekly. 世田谷's 「コーデ騎士団」 runs one-coin cafe-bars as places for senior men (Cabinet Office case book). No revenue data was found. [S] — [onaji.me 2025-09-08](https://onaji.me/entry/2025/09/08); [内閣府 高齢者の居場所と出番 事例集](https://www8.cao.go.jp/kourei/ishiki/h23/kenkyu/gaiyo/pdf/1_2.pdf)

### Inferences
- Men 60+ are the least-served segment. Japan offers mostly public and volunteer models. A paid "host-led conversation + light activity" format (the 昼スナック pattern, run in borrowed off-hours space) is a low-capex experiment a Korean team could test.
- For online learning, the 5060 opportunity may be more on the supply side (seniors as teachers, which gives income and 生きがい) than demand.
- Smartphone or AI literacy classes work best as acquisition and onboarding funnels (a carrier model), not as revenue.

### Gaps
- No NHK/朝日 culture center figures after FY2022, and no age mix by percentage.
- No au/SoftBank smartphone class totals were found.
- No ストアカ learner age breakdown in percent was found.

## 5. Senior dating / partner search (シニア婚活)

### Takeaway
Demand for marriage and partners among people 50+ is growing fast in Japan's agency channel: IBJ 50+ marriages rose about 4x in 8 years (348 → 1,389). Specialist agencies such as 茜会 are small (about 4,000 members) and event-heavy. Mainstream apps hold 50s users at roughly 10–20% (maribish-style "バツイチ" positioning skews older), but the data is from affiliate sites and unreliable.

### Cited Findings
- **IBJ (largest marriage-agency network):** marriages by members 50+ went 348 (2017) → 1,030 (2023) → 1,241 (2024) → **1,389 (2025, record)**. The 2025 figure is 520 women and 869 men. Women grew about 5.8x and men about 3.4x vs 2017. 50+ sign-ups roughly doubled on remarriage demand (成婚白書 2024). [S] — [IBJ結婚みらい研究所](https://www.ibjapan.jp/mirai-lab/seikon-hakusho/871/); [PR TIMES IBJ 2026-07](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000998.000007950.html); [IBJ 成婚白書 2024年度版 PDF](https://www.ibjapan.jp/information/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024IBJseikon_hakusyo.pdf)
- **茜会 (中高年専門):** founding is given as "1960" or "65+ years", depending on the source. Members are about 4,000 (comparison sites; not officially disclosed). Average age is reported as men 62.7 / women 59.9, or men 59.3 / women 56.7 (sources conflict). About 70% are in their 50s–60s. It runs 500+ matchmaking parties a year. Comparison sites are affiliate and ad-driven, so the reliability is low. [S] — [kekkon-ouen](https://www.kekkon-ouen.net/articles/senior/article_201/); [lifrell](https://lifrell.co.jp/marriage/approaching-the-truth-about-akanekai/); [createmyevent 2026](https://createmyevent.com/entry237.html)
- **Apps (50s share; affiliate-site data, inconsistent):** マリッシュ is put at about 20%, or women 13% / men 18%, or men 39% / women 28% (possible data mix-up with Omiai). ペアーズ has 25M cumulative members with 50s under 10% (but more than 10k per gender). ユーブライド is about 10–13%, with another tally at 23%. [S] — [marri-marri 年齢層 2026-08](https://marri-marri.jp/media/machingapp/age/); [laskoi 2026](https://laskoi.jp/blog/post/matchingapp-agerange)
- No reliable data was found on the size of the senior dating market or on 事実婚 (common-law) trends among seniors. IBJ data covers only legal-marriage exits. [S] — same IBJ sources (caveat)

### Inferences
- Japan's 50+ partner demand is being captured by high-trust, high-fee human-mediated agencies (ID-verified, counselor-led) and parties, not by apps. Trust and safety (romance scams, married users) is the key barrier for older users.
- Women's 50+ demand is growing faster than men's. A Korean product could design for a female-led trust experience (verified profiles, group-first events).
- This lines up with Korea's 시놀 growth through rotation dating and group meetings (see section 8).

### Gaps
- No official age breakdowns from Pairs, maribish or youbride (IR or press) were found.
- No 事実婚 / パートナーシップ data for seniors, and no お見合いパーティー 50代 market data (participants, pricing), were found.

## 6. Lifestyle commerce and second careers (シニア向けEC, D2C, セカンドキャリア, 顧問マッチング)

### Takeaway
Senior commerce in Japan is dominated by catalog and paper-ad D2C players (通販生活/カタログハウス ¥26.9bn, 世田谷自然食品 about ¥30bn est., やずや about ¥17bn est., down from a ¥40bn+ peak). The health-food mail-order market is now flat to declining. Second-career services are growing (シニアジョブ listings up 7x in 27 months), but most data is self-reported.

### Cited Findings
- **カタログハウス (通販生活):** FY3/2025 revenue ¥26.92bn (+12%), from repeat products and more catalog issues (quarterly → bimonthly, 6x/yr). Operating profit turned negative on increased ad spend for new customers. [S] — [日本流通産業新聞 3127](https://online.bci.co.jp/article/detail/3127); [nifty/日流](https://news.nifty.com/article/economy/economyall/12399-4268879/)
- Health-food mail order (日本流通産業新聞 ranking): the 2025 edition covers 118 companies at ¥113.5bn total, with real growth of −1.1% among 19 comparable firms. The 2026 edition shows 111 companies at ¥697.8bn (different scope, not comparable). Another source puts the market at more than ¥500bn. [S] — [日流 2025版](https://www.bci.co.jp/nichiryu/article/17065); [日流 2026版](https://online.bci.co.jp/article/detail/5004); [NetIB](https://www.data-max.co.jp/article/33769)
- 世田谷自然食品 is about ¥30bn (est.), led by lactic-acid aojiru and sold through print ads to seniors. やずや is about ¥17bn (est.), down from over ¥40bn at its 香酢 peak. [S] — [netkeizai 2024 ranking](https://netkeizai.com/articles/detail/11178); [rpst](https://rpst.jp/blog/marketing_analysis/15109/)
- **シニアジョブ (50+ jobs):** cumulative staffing and placement registrants are 89k+, job-site registrants 13k+ (2024 interview). Listings grew 7x+ from December 2023 to March 2026. Employers pay only on hire. [S] — [時事/PR](https://www.jiji.com/jc/article?k=000000194.000031414&g=prt); [キャリアビジョン 2024](https://career-vision.or.jp/20240522-2)
- **ビザスク:** 600k+ registrants after the Coleman acquisition (800k+ across 190 countries per another listing) and about 60k matches a year. No 50+ share was found. [S] — [green-japan](https://www.green-japan.com/company/4275); [careercross](https://www.careercross.com/job/pdf-1598864)

### Inferences
- Senior D2C acquisition still relies on paper channels (newspaper inserts, catalogs). Rising acquisition costs pushed カタログハウス into losses. Owning a trusted media relationship (as Halmek does) is the moat.
- Second-career demand is real, but monetization is placement fees (B2B). It could be an adjacent revenue line for a 5060 community, through referrals or "advisor" matching.

### Gaps
- ビザスク 50+ share, シニア起業 platform data, and 定年前 career-service market size were not found.
- Health-food D2C 50+ customer share was not found.

## 7. Which failed or stayed small, and why

### Takeaway
No Japanese senior-only SNS broke out of the hundreds-of-thousands range on organic growth. The big numbers belong to services with a core utility or a distribution channel (rail discounts, handsets, tours, a subscription magazine). The documented failure causes are weak customer understanding by young product teams, ad-only revenue with high fixed costs, and aging communities with no renewal.

### Cited Findings
- Nikkei Xtrend on failed senior services: setting the business domain as "中高年・シニア向け" raises uncertainty, and the largest cause is poor grasp of seniors' realities. Young developers have little contact with the target, and customer-led development is recommended. [S] — [日経クロストレンド「届いたお叱りの声 失敗したシニア向けサービスの原因とは」](https://xtrend.nikkei.com/atcl/contents/18/00672/00005/)
- Aging community services (gooブログ, 教えて!goo) closed after users aged and stopped renewing, with ad-dependent revenue and high system costs. [S] — [Walkers note gooブログ](https://note.com/app_dev_lab/n/n75e386d92d3b); [Walkers note 教えて!goo](https://note.com/app_dev_lab/n/n6697b39f7313)
- 趣味人倶楽部 grew to only about 400k–420k members over about 18 years (2007 → 2025). [S] — [Excite/PR TIMES 2025](https://www.excite.co.jp/news/article/Prtimes_2025-02-13-18508-143/)
- Culture-center revenue was stagnant even as the senior share rose. [S] — [日経業界解説](https://www.nikkei.com/telecom/industry_s/0893)
- The feature-phone version of らくらくコミュニティ was closed (2017) during the platform shift. [S] — [FCNT](https://www.fcnt.com/consumernotice/1016/)

### Inferences
- Pattern: "senior" as a label repels the target. Successful brands frame around life-stage values (ハルメク = "前向きに生きる50代からの女性", 大人の休日) rather than age.
- Scale requires piggybacking on an existing high-frequency or high-trust relationship. Pure community apps stay small and must monetize through events, travel, commerce or B2B research.

### Gaps
- No detailed post-mortems of specific senior SNSs (for example, DeNA's exit from 趣味人倶楽部, or the Mixi-era senior communities) were found.

## 8. Korea comparison (brief): 시놀, 퀸잇, 오팔 and gaps

### Takeaway
Korea's 5060 space is early. 시놀 (Sinor) grew to about 110k members (June 2026) through dating and group meetings plus a travel membership, with revenue in the hundreds of millions of won. 퀸잇 (40–50s women's fashion) is the commerce scale player (₩85.6bn in 2025, still loss-making). No Korean player combines a trusted subscription content brand, commerce and community the way Halmek does.

### Cited Findings
- **시놀:** targets ages from the early 50s to the late 70s, combining social dating with hobby and leisure matching. It had about 10k users shortly after launch. Members were 20k (2024) → 90k (March 2025, with ₩60M monthly revenue reported) → 100k (August 2025) → about 110k (June 2026). TheVC estimates revenue at ₩46M (2023) → ₩197M → ₩403M (2025). The ₩60M/month figure conflicts with TheVC. It launched a group-meeting "3-pack" (August 2025) and a 5060 travel membership "같이 여행 갈 친구" (July 2026). [S] — [월간 리크루트](http://www.hkrecruit.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=26489); [네이트 2025-03](https://news.nate.com/view/20250309n02943); [네이트 2025-08](https://news.nate.com/view/20250829n30147); [머니투데이 2026-07-21](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2026/07/21/2026072109271921364); [TheVC](https://thevc.kr/sinor); [네이트 2026-07 로테이션 소개팅](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260705n13485)
- 당근 (Daangn): 5060 groups created through June 2026 rose 300%+ YoY. [S] — [네이트 2026-07](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260705n13485)
- **퀸잇 (라포랩스):** 2025 revenue ₩85.6bn (+20%+), operating loss reduced to ₩5.9bn. 2024 revenue was about ₩71.1bn. [S] — [한국일보](https://daily.hankooki.com/news/articleViewAmp.html?idxno=1373564); [네이트 2025-06](https://news.nate.com/view/20250617n03646)
- 캐시워크 ranked 4th in app share among 5060 users (reward app as a distribution channel). [S] — [이투데이](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2589404)
- **오팔:** no reliable information found on a Korean service by this name. "오팔(OPAL, Old People with Active Lives)" is commonly used in Korea as a generational term, not a product (background, unverified).

### Inferences
- Gaps in Korea compared with Japan:
  - There is no trusted 5060 women's content brand with a paid subscription and commerce, as Halmek has.
  - There is no Club Tourism-scale themed solo-travel operator with community built in.
  - There is no handset or carrier-bundled senior community like らくらくコミュニティ.
  - There is no scaled agency-style verified matchmaking specific to 50+, apart from early 시놀.
- Japan's lessons point a Korean team toward several choices:
  - Lead with a concrete paid activity (trips, classes, group meetings) rather than open SNS.
  - Build trust and verification in, especially for dating.
  - Treat community as the retention layer for commerce or travel revenue.
  - Find a distribution partner (carrier, bank, 당근, 캐시워크).

### Gaps
- 시놀's funding, unit economics and retention were not found.
- Other Korean 5060 community, dating or learning players (for example, 5060-specific class platforms) were not surveyed here.
